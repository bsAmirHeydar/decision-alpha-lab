# Danger Zones

## Purpose

Danger zones show the pre/post news window around red or breaking events.

Default:

```text
15 minutes before
15 minutes after
```

## Render Rule

Only these events produce zones:

- High impact events.
- Breaking events.

## Trading Doctrine

The zone is not a signal. It is a risk-context overlay. The tool does not tell the trader to buy or sell.
