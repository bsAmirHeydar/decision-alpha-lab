# Hook After Opposing F3

A Hook After Opposing F3 is a production-valid Hook family.

## Definition

A Hook is valid by the F3 rule when:

1. the latest relevant F3 is opposite to the Hook direction;
2. the F3 has formed its flag / F3 structure;
3. the market is still in or extending from that F3 environment;
4. the Hook is born from the terminal side of that opposing F3.

## Example

```text
Latest F3 = bullish F3
A negative Hook forms from the terminal/top side of that bullish F3
=> valid Hook After Opposing F3
```

## Important nuance

F3 may lock at one point, but the market can continue inside the F3 environment. Therefore, the Hook after F3 is not every later Hook after an old F3. It is the Hook born from the terminal F3 area.

## Label prefix

Recommended label prefix:

```text
F3H
```
