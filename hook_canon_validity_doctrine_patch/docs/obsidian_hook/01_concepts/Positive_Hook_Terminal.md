# Positive Hook Terminal

## Definition

The terminal of a positive Hook is the lowest valley reached that remains above the Hook origin/start boundary.

```text
positive_hook_terminal = lowest valley above origin
```

For cycle drawing, the endpoint may use the lowest raw low associated with that terminal area, as long as the origin boundary has not been breached before terminal confirmation.

## Why this matters

A positive Hook cycle must not stop early merely because a sequence label ended at `2` or `3`. The cycle must reach the actual terminal/death-near extreme of the Hook.
