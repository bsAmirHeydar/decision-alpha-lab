# Hard Rules

1. A potential area without a stable stop is not an execution zone.
2. A broad F2 or F3 area requires lower-timeframe refinement before execution.
3. A zone must be mechanically derived from context, not visually guessed.
4. The system must estimate potential versus cost before allowing execution.
5. Limit entry is preferred; chasing after convexity is gone is prohibited.
6. The primary learning objective must not be raw win rate.
7. Every zone touch must be logged.
8. Every stop must be interpreted as zone expiration or as evidence for model improvement.
9. No live automation without hard risk controls and audit logs.
10. No fake pattern trading.
