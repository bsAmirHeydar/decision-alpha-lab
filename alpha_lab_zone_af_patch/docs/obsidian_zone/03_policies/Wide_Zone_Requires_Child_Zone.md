# Wide Zone Requires Child Zone

Wide zones are not automatically invalid. They are expensive.

Policy:

```text
If the parent zone is meaningful but too wide, search for a child zone.
If no child zone appears, classify as watch zone.
If a child zone appears with clear entry and stop, execute from the child risk contract.
```

This policy is central to the fractal architecture.
