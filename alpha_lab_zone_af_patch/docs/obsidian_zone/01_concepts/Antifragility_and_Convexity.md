# Antifragility and Convexity

The system is antifragile when bounded failures become structured information and help improve future zone selection.

The system is convex when the loss side is limited and the reward side can expand beyond the initial risk.

## Practical Meaning

- Small stop-outs are acceptable if they are predefined.
- Wide undefined losses are unacceptable.
- Win rate is secondary until convexity is validated.
- Tail capture and MFE/MAE matter more than immediate prediction accuracy.
