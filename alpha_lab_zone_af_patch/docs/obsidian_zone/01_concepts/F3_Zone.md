# F3 Zone

F3 creates a broad reversal environment. It does not define a precise start or stop for the zone on its own.

## Core Policy

F3 is not a direct execution zone unless a lower-timeframe child zone defines:

- stable entry;
- stable stop;
- acceptable width;
- enough open reward potential.
