# Zone as Risk Contract

A zone is a price range where the system can define:

```text
entry edge + stop edge + open reward path
```

The entry edge buys access to potential. The stop edge expires the thesis. The reward side is not fixed by default; it is grown when the market releases energy.

## Key Rule

A potential area without a stop edge is not an execution zone.
