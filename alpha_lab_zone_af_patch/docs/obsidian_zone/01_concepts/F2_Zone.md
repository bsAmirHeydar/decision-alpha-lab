# F2 Zone

F2 can create reversal zones, but many F2 areas lack a stable stop on the parent timeframe.

## Cases

1. Point 1 and 2 form without hitting the F2 waist.
2. Point 1 and 2 form and the F2 waist is also hit.
3. F2 exceeds symmetry with F1 and creates broad reversal potential.

## Core Policy

If the stop is not stable, F2 is a watch zone or parent zone, not a direct execution zone.

Lower-timeframe refinement is usually required.
