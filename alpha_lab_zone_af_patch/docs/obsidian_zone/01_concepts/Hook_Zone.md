# Hook Zone

A Hook Zone is created by near-death hook logic and symmetry.

## Execution Logic

```text
Entry area = near-death / symmetry-derived hook area
Stop area  = behind the hook
```

Hook Zones are among the cleaner zone types because they often provide a stable stop.

## Requirements

- valid hook structure;
- symmetry or near-death area;
- stop behind hook;
- acceptable width;
- open reward path;
- lower-timeframe refinement when the hook zone is too wide.
