# F1 Zone

After F1 forms, the flag is taken, and points 1 and 2 are made, the system can search for entry in the direction of F1 from point 2.

## Risk Contract

```text
Entry area = point 2 / pullback area in F1 direction
Stop area  = behind the waist of F1
```

The closer price comes to the F1 waist, the narrower the zone can become.
