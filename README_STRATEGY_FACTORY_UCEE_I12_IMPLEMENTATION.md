# UCEE I12 — Unified Statistical, Anti-Overfit, and Promotion Governance Gate

UCE-I12 consumes the immutable UCE-I11 experiment manifest and complete selection ledger and produces a signed, reproducible `reject`, `challenge`, or `promote` decision. It counts the full declared search surface—including failed, skipped, invalid, pruned, timed-out, cancelled, selected, ensembled, and manually overridden choices—and makes critical leakage, integrity, selection, multiplicity, parity, and safety failures non-compensatory.

## Delivered surfaces

- Dependence-aware effective sample size, moving-block bootstrap, cluster bootstrap, paired comparisons, subgroup intervals, and sequential confidence.
- Nested-selection overlap audit and locked confirmatory declarations.
- Bonferroni, Holm, Benjamini-Hochberg, and Benjamini-Yekutieli correction across exact family definitions.
- PBO/CSCV, deflated performance, White reality check, SPA-style testing, and complete trial-universe reconciliation.
- Matched-time, matched-context, random-direction, label-permutation, placebo, delayed-trigger, random-treatment, and mandatory manual-baseline controls.
- Parameter, seed, fold, regime, session, symbol, cost, spread, slippage, latency, fill, trade-drop, best-trade, tail, capacity, and source-revision stress contracts.
- Brier score, log loss, reliability/ECE, conformal coverage, decision curves, utility-by-threshold, abstention coverage, and risk-tier calibration.
- Non-compensatory model-risk scorecard and prospective challenge freeze.
- Canonical evidence bundle with detached HMAC-SHA256 reference signing boundary and artifact-integrity verification.
- 25 closed JSON schemas, seven suite descriptors, deterministic conformance vectors, and 11 MQL5 contract/diagnostic files.
- 34 detailed delivery chapters and eight atomic Obsidian concepts.

## Authority boundary

This phase has no broker, order, position, sizing, live-network, or trading authority. A `promote` decision only admits an artifact to UCE-I13 bounded policy composition. It does not authorize execution.
