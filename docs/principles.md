# Research Principles

- Price is the only directly observable market truth.
- Structural memory is preferred over fixed temporal assumptions.
- The market x-axis is a sequence of decision nodes and known-time events, not arbitrary windows.
- A label can only influence a decision after it becomes knowable.
- Same-candle labels are simultaneous, not sequential.
- Samples are useful for discovery, but official live claims require no-sample or strictly causal replay.
- Reversal and continuation must be reported separately.
- Path-normalized R must not be confused with execution R.
- Simplicity is preferred over unnecessary complexity.
- Prediction is secondary to adaptation.
- Convexity is more valuable than raw accuracy.
- Negative results are valuable results.
- Alpha claims require empirical evidence under matched baselines.
- Every experiment must be reproducible.
