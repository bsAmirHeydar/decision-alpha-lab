# Glossary

Decision Node:
A structural location where the market was previously forced to choose.

Decision Energy:
The strength associated with a market decision, inferred from observable behavior.

Momentum:
Self-reinforcing market dynamics.

Mean Reversion:
Self-correcting market dynamics.

Regime:
The dominant interaction between momentum and mean-reverting forces.

Convexity:
A payoff structure characterized by limited downside and open-ended upside.
