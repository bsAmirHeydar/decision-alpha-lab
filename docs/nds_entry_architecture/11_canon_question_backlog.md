# NDS Entry Canon Question Backlog

## A. Structure-to-opportunity

1. Does every valid Hook generate a Zone candidate?
2. Can a closed historical Hook remain a Zone source after origin breach?
3. Does Hook-after-Hook or post-F3 family carry intrinsic setup priority?
4. Is Hook direction identical to trade direction, opposite, or context-dependent?
5. Can one Hook create multiple Setup families?

## B. Zone geometry

1. Which points define lower and upper boundaries for positive and negative Hooks?
2. Is geometry wick-, close-, node-, or body-based?
3. Is padding fixed, volatility-based, or anatomy-based?
4. What are minimum and maximum widths?
5. Does terminal improvement revise the Zone or create a new revision?
6. How does parent/child refinement work?

## C. Zone lifecycle

1. Birth time?
2. Activation condition?
3. First-touch versus first-entry consumption?
4. Touch-count weakening?
5. Wick versus close invalidation?
6. Time expiry?
7. Opposing-structure invalidation?
8. Re-entry after reaction?

## D. Entry

1. First edge, midpoint, near-death edge, confirmation, or ladder?
2. Limit, stop, market, or hybrid order?
3. Is fast in-and-out interaction valid?
4. Is entry allowed before Zone activation?
5. What is the maximum acceptable spread/slippage relative to Zone width?

## E. Stop and invalidation

1. Zone opposite edge, origin, death boundary, or another point?
2. Wick or close semantics for thesis invalidation?
3. Broker stop buffer?
4. Emergency stop distinct from thesis stop?
5. Gap policy?

## F. Target and management

1. Is the primary destination crown, origin, next structure, fixed R, or open-ended?
2. Are targets diagnostic only while profit remains structurally managed?
3. Partial exits?
4. Time exit?
5. Movement-quality exit?

## G. Risk and portfolio

1. Risk budget per Setup?
2. Multiple simultaneous Zones?
3. family-level exposure cap?
4. correlated instruments?
5. daily stop and kill switch?
6. child-Zone risk allocation versus parent-Zone risk?

Each answer must update the Canon, adapter, state machine, dataset schema, and test matrix together.
