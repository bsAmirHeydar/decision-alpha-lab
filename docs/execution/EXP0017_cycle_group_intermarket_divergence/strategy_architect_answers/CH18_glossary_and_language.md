# CH18 — Glossary and Language

## Execution Freedom

The base doctrine that a confirmed, non-invalidated divergence may be executed without additional position, direction, CG, or hedge restrictions.

## No-Limit Execution

A research-state execution model where all valid signals are allowed so the full raw behavior of the strategy can be measured.

## Position Limit

A rule that caps total positions, positions per CG, positions per symbol, positions per direction, or positions per time window. Chapter 18 does not activate such a rule.

## Same-CG Position

A position generated by a signal from the same cycle group as another active or prior signal.

## Repeated Opportunity

A later valid signal from the same CG, same symbol, same direction, or same day after another signal has already occurred.

## Post-Loss Signal

A valid signal that occurs after a prior loss. Chapter 18 does not block it.

## Post-Win Signal

A valid signal that occurs after a prior win. Chapter 18 does not treat it differently.

## Hedge

Coexistence of positions that create opposite directional exposure. Chapter 18 allows it in the base layer.

## Conflict

A situation where valid signals point in different directions or belong to different CGs. Chapter 18 records it rather than resolving it manually.

## Position Cluster

A group of open positions that overlap in time and may share CG, direction, symbol, or risk exposure.

## Cluster Risk

The drawdown or exposure created by multiple open positions existing together.

## Final Confirmation

The moment after the active candle closes and the divergence remains valid.

## True Invalidation

Loss of intermarket asymmetry because both symbols have hunted their corresponding references.

## Future Constraint

A restriction that may be introduced later only if statistical testing proves it improves the strategy.

## Raw Execution Field

The full set of confirmed and non-invalidated signals before filters, caps, ranking, or constraint logic.
