# CH08 — Hypothesis Register

## Purpose

This register converts Chapter 08 answers into testable strategic hypotheses. These are not rules yet. They are structured possibilities to be studied later.

## H08-01 — Candle Close Improves Signal Quality

**Hypothesis:** Divergences confirmed after candle close may perform better than raw intrabar hunt observations.

**Reasoning:** Waiting for close ensures the active behavioral unit is complete.

**What to observe:**

- win rate after confirmation,
- expectancy after confirmation,
- invalidation rate after confirmation,
- movement quality after confirmation,
- comparison with raw intrabar entries.

## H08-02 — Close Confirmation Is Not Late

**Hypothesis:** Waiting for the candle close does not meaningfully reduce the opportunity quality.

**Reasoning:** The strategy architect views close as permission, not delay.

**What to observe:**

- missed movement before confirmation,
- movement remaining after confirmation,
- risk/reward after confirmation,
- slippage between hunt and confirmation,
- post-confirmation expansion.

## H08-03 — Temporal Close Is Enough

**Hypothesis:** A time-based candle close is sufficient; the candle does not need a special close-location rule.

**Reasoning:** The base doctrine says only time close matters.

**What to observe:**

- close beyond reference vs not beyond reference,
- body position,
- wick position,
- candle color,
- close near high/low,
- outcome difference across those families.

## H08-04 — Double Hunt Cancels Divergence

**Hypothesis:** When both symbols hunt the corresponding reference, the original divergence edge disappears.

**Reasoning:** Divergence requires asymmetry. Double hunt removes asymmetry.

**What to observe:**

- outcomes after double hunt,
- continuation after double hunt,
- reversal after double hunt,
- whether double hunt has its own non-divergence meaning,
- whether double hunt should be tracked separately.

## H08-05 — All Confirmed Signals Should Be Preserved Before Ranking

**Hypothesis:** Preserving all confirmed divergences produces better statistical discovery than manually filtering or hiding signals early.

**Reasoning:** Early filtering may destroy the dataset and hide useful families.

**What to observe:**

- signal clusters,
- same-time multi-CG confirmations,
- conflict between CGs,
- overlap between references,
- performance of signals that would have been manually ignored.

## H08-06 — Confirmation Timing Inside Cycle Matters

**Hypothesis:** A confirmed divergence may perform differently depending on where it confirms inside the active CG cycle.

**Reasoning:** Early-cycle, mid-cycle, and late-cycle confirmations may have different remaining time and pressure.

**What to observe:**

- confirmation near cycle start,
- confirmation near cycle middle,
- confirmation near cycle end,
- movement remaining until cycle end,
- invalidation timing.

## H08-07 — Uniform Confirmation Rule Prevents Bias

**Hypothesis:** Applying the same confirmation rule to all cycle groups and directions creates a cleaner research foundation.

**Reasoning:** Different confirmation rules before testing would inject unmeasured assumptions.

**What to observe:**

- whether later statistics justify specialized confirmation rules,
- whether some CGs need different confirmation horizons,
- whether uniformity remains optimal after data review.

## H08-08 — Trade Permission Does Not Equal Forced Allocation

**Hypothesis:** A confirmed divergence can create trade permission while later risk governance may still decide whether to allocate capital.

**Reasoning:** Signal validity and portfolio allocation are different questions.

**What to observe:**

- simultaneous confirmations,
- exposure concentration,
- conflicting signals,
- overtrading risk,
- performance with all valid signals vs selected valid signals.

## Register Status

All hypotheses remain open until statistical study provides evidence.
