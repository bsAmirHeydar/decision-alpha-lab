## 2. Strategic meaning

This chapter creates the **epistemic discipline** of the strategy.

The system is allowed to ask:

- Is this CG better?
- Is this symbol role better?
- Is this direction better?
- Is this timing better?
- Is this reference distance better?
- Is this signal shape better?
- Is this invalidation behavior meaningful?
- Is this future idea useful?

But the system is not allowed to answer these questions through visual preference, intuition, story, or isolated examples before statistical testing.

## 3. The doctrine of unknown-before-tested

The default state of every proposed distinction is:

```text
UNKNOWN -> TESTABLE -> CLASSIFIABLE -> MAYBE RULE
```

Not:

```text
UNKNOWN -> OPINION -> RULE
```

This is central. It prevents strategy pollution.

## 4. No manual quality before data

A valid divergence is not manually made better or worse because of:

- larger CG size;
- smaller CG size;
- specific time window;
- cash-session location;
- outside-cash location;
- SPXUSD as hunter;
- NDXUSD as hunter;
- SPXUSD as clean symbol;
- NDXUSD as clean symbol;
- buy direction;
- sell direction;
- near reference;
- far reference;
- multiple references;
- visual sharpness;
- speed of reaction;
- market news;
- recent win/loss sequence;
- previous-day behavior.

Before testing, these are only descriptive fields. They are not quality labels.

## 5. Equal base validity

At the base layer, if the core divergence condition exists and is not invalidated, it remains valid.

Base validity means:

- the signal exists;
- the signal is recordable;
- the signal is comparable;
- the signal is allowed into the statistical field;
- the signal is not manually suppressed.

Base validity does **not** mean:

- the signal is profitable;
- the signal is high quality;
- the signal deserves more risk;
- the signal deserves priority;
- the signal should survive future filtering.

## 6. Difference between not knowing and not caring

The strategy does not say that differences do not exist.

It says:

> We do not know which differences matter yet.

This distinction matters because it keeps the research open while keeping the execution doctrine clean.

The architect is not dismissing potential differences. He is refusing to convert them into rules prematurely.

## 7. Future ideas are not rejected

Question 10 is important. The answer says:

> We do not do this now, but after statistical testing this idea may be useful.

Therefore, future ideas are not destroyed. They are placed into an idea backlog.

The proper lifecycle is:

1. preserve the idea;
2. do not activate it in the base model;
3. collect raw statistics first;
4. test whether the idea has measurable value;
5. only then decide whether it becomes a filter, weighting, model feature, or separate setup family.

## 8. Main strategic law

> No untested distinction may change the validity, risk, priority, visibility, or execution permission of a raw confirmed divergence.

## 9. What this chapter protects

This chapter protects the project from:

- overfitting;
- narrative fitting;
- cherry-picking;
- manual bias;
- premature optimization;
- signal suppression;
- unstable rule creation;
- visual mythology around cycle groups;
- false hierarchy between CGs;
- trader preference disguised as edge.

## 10. What this chapter enables

It enables:

- clean datasets;
- fair statistical testing;
- honest signal comparison;
- future model building;
- AI-ready feature fields;
- discovery instead of assumption;
- modular strategy evolution.

## 11. Locked doctrine

The chapter locks this principle:

> Until statistical testing proves otherwise, every proposed behavioral difference remains neutral and every raw valid divergence remains part of the research field.
