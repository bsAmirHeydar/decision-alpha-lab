# CH18 — Hypothesis Register

## H18-01 — No-Limit Execution May Be Profitable

The unrestricted field of all confirmed non-invalidated divergences may have acceptable win rate and expectancy.

## H18-02 — No-Limit Execution May Create Risk Clusters

Even if individual signals have edge, unlimited simultaneous exposure may create unacceptable drawdown clusters.

## H18-03 — Same-CG Repetition May Be Valuable

Multiple signals from the same CG may represent repeated valid opportunities rather than overtrading.

## H18-04 — Same-CG Repetition May Be Dangerous

Repeated signals from the same CG may represent noise, exhaustion, or overexposure.

## H18-05 — Post-Loss Signals May Still Work

A failed cycle does not necessarily reduce the quality of the next signal from the same CG.

## H18-06 — Post-Loss Signals May Need Future Filtering

If statistics show that signals after recent stop-outs perform poorly, a future cooldown rule may be considered.

## H18-07 — Hedging May Be Useful

Opposite-direction positions may preserve independent CG information and improve total outcome.

## H18-08 — Hedging May Be Harmful

Opposite-direction positions may create unnecessary cost, conflict, drawdown, or reduced clarity.

## H18-09 — Immediate Entry May Be Optimal

Entering immediately after final confirmation may capture the cleanest edge.

## H18-10 — Immediate Entry May Be Improved Later

Future studies may show that delay, pullback, or retest entries improve outcomes, but this does not change the base doctrine now.

## H18-11 — No Position Limit May Need Future Caps

If exposure clusters produce excessive drawdown, a future maximum-position rule may be introduced after statistical proof.

## H18-12 — Constraint-Free Base Layer Improves Research Integrity

Preserving all signals before applying constraints may prevent overfitting and allow more accurate discovery of signal-family behavior.
