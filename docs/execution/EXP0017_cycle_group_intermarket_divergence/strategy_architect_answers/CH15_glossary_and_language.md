# CH15 — Glossary and Language

## Actionable signal

A confirmed divergence that has reached trade permission after the confirmation candle closes and the clean-symbol condition remains valid.

## Primary sample

The main statistical population used to evaluate strategy performance. In Chapter 15, this means confirmed and tradeable signals only.

## Win rate

The percentage of signals that avoid loss and finish as winners under the selected result definition.

## Stop rate

The percentage of signals that hit the stop or fail through the defined risk boundary.

## Risk-to-reward

The relationship between the risk taken from entry to stop and the reward produced after confirmation.

## Pip / point movement

The raw movement of the clean symbol after the signal, measured in market units rather than emotional or visual terms.

## Daily-range-normalized movement

A quality index where signal movement is divided by the total movement of that trading day. It measures how much of the day's opportunity the signal captured.

## Dollar outcome

The actual profit or loss contribution of the signal in money terms.

## Cycle-group overlap

A condition where more than one cycle group produces valid or related divergence information around the same market behavior.

## Signal frequency

The number of confirmed signals generated over a day, week, month, CG family, direction, symbol role, or time window.

## Signal density

The concentration of signals in a limited period. High density may indicate opportunity or noise and must be tested.

## Confirmed sample

A sample made of signals that passed the candle-close confirmation requirement.

## Emotional language

Non-measurable language such as beautiful, ugly, scary, attractive, or strong by feeling. Chapter 15 removes this language from reports.

## Measurable language

Language based on observable or calculable outcomes: win rate, R, pip movement, normalized movement, dollar outcome, stop streak, overlap count, and sample size.

## Report memory

The statistical record of the strategy. It allows later classification, filtering, weighting, and model-building.

## Recommended field

A statistical field suggested for future reports even if the strategy architect did not explicitly define it, because it can help classify signal behavior.
