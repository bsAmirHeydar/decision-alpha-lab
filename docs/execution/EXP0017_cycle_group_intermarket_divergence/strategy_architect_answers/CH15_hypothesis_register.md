# CH15 — Hypothesis Register

## H15-01 — Win rate as an early health indicator

Hypothesis: signal families with higher win rate and fewer stop-outs may be more suitable for base execution than families with weak survival behavior.

Status: test required.

## H15-02 — R:R reveals hidden value beyond win rate

Hypothesis: some families may have lower win rate but strong reward, and therefore still produce acceptable expectancy.

Status: test required.

## H15-03 — Pip movement is different from dollar outcome

Hypothesis: raw movement in pips or points may reveal market behavior that dollar result alone hides.

Status: test required.

## H15-04 — Daily-range-normalized movement is a key quality index

Hypothesis: pip movement divided by total daily range may show which signal families capture a meaningful part of the day.

Status: priority test.

## H15-05 — Cycle-group type changes report meaning

Hypothesis: every CG family may show different sample count, win rate, movement, invalidation behavior, or frequency after enough data.

Status: test required.

## H15-06 — Cycle-group overlap may change signal quality

Hypothesis: overlap between multiple CG signals may improve, reduce, or change trade quality depending on direction, timing, and symbol role.

Status: test required.

## H15-07 — Signal frequency changes market condition

Hypothesis: days, weeks, or months with unusually high signal count may behave differently from normal-density periods.

Status: test required.

## H15-08 — Only confirmed actionable signals should define primary expectancy

Hypothesis: using only confirmed and tradeable signals produces a cleaner performance sample than mixing raw hunts and unconfirmed candidates.

Status: base doctrine; can be challenged later through secondary archives.

## H15-09 — Emotional labels reduce research quality

Hypothesis: removing emotional language improves the clarity and discipline of statistical evaluation.

Status: base doctrine.

## H15-10 — Additional recommended measures improve later model-building

Hypothesis: fields such as maximum favorable movement, maximum adverse movement, time to profit, time to stop, overlap count, and signal density will improve future statistical classification and AI scoring.

Status: recommended research extension.
