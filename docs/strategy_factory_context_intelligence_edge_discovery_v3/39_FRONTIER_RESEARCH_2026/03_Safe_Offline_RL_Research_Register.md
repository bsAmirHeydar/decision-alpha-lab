# Review questions

- Finite action support must be declared and independently challenged.
- Behavior coverage must be declared and independently challenged.
- Reward/cost validity must be declared and independently challenged.
- Ope disagreement must be declared and independently challenged.
- Pessimism must be declared and independently challenged.
- Deployment safety projection must be declared and independently challenged.

## Decision rule

No paper or benchmark result grants production authority. It can only motivate a preregistered governed-challenger experiment.
