# Review questions

- Exchangeability or weighted/online alternative must be declared and independently challenged.
- Calibration sample size must be declared and independently challenged.
- Loss monotonicity must be declared and independently challenged.
- Selection interaction must be declared and independently challenged.
- Subgroup coverage must be declared and independently challenged.
- Nonstationarity response must be declared and independently challenged.

## Decision rule

No paper or benchmark result grants production authority. It can only motivate a preregistered governed-challenger experiment.
