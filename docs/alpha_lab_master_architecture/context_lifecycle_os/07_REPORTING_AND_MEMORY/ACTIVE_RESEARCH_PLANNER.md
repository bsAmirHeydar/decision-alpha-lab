# Active Research Planner

The Active Research Planner begins in ACL-09. ACL-08 may emit bounded evidence questions derived from UNKNOWN gates, but it does not prioritize, schedule, budget or execute experiments.

Planning must account for duplicate research, decision-relevant uncertainty, expected information value, resource budget and policy constraints. A report finding cannot create an experiment without a separate permit and frozen batch.
