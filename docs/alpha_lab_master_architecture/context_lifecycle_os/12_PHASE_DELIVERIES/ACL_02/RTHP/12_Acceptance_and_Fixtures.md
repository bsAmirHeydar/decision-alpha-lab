# 12 Acceptance and Fixtures

Acceptance requires positive, negative, and ambiguous fixtures covering unilateral High and Low touches, bilateral touch, no touch, stale synchronized data, price-basis mismatch, partial and zero history, restart deduplication, and sequential/non-sequential M15 relationships.

A fixture failure is non-compensatory. Aggregate success cannot waive a failed semantic or authority boundary.
