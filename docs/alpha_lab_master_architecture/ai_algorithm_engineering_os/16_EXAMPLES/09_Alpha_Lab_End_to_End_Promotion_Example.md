# Alpha Lab End-to-End Promotion Example

A divergence observation becomes a hypothesis, then an experiment with causal reference lifecycle, outcome dataset, statistical reports, fixed walk-forward folds, controlled model comparison, and a human promotion decision. Until the promotion artifact exists, rankings and models remain research evidence and cannot place orders.
