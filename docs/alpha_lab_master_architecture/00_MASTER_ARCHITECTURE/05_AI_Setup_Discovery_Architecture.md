# AI Setup Discovery Architecture

## 1. Purpose

AI is used to discover how a defined context can be exploited, not to invent market meaning without boundaries. Human doctrine narrows the semantic world; AI searches the exploitation world.

## 2. Inputs

- immutable context package;
- fixed and versioned representations;
- allowed treatment atoms and compatibility graph;
- causal outcome cube;
- executable economic costs;
- data roles and folds;
- objective profile and hard guardrails;
- search budget;
- prior research memory permitted for this lineage.

## 3. Learning Tasks

The trainer system supports a ladder:

1. unconditional treatment baselines;
2. simple deterministic filters;
3. interpretable linear/probabilistic models;
4. tree and boosting models;
5. candidate ranking and treatment choice;
6. sequence, graph and multi-view models;
7. regime experts and ensembles;
8. constrained offline policy learning;
9. post-entry adaptation only after simpler tasks are proven.

Complexity must earn measurable, protected uplift.

## 4. Treatment Search

The search space is compiled and pruned before expensive training:

- semantic incompatibility;
- causality failure;
- invalid geometry;
- broker infeasibility;
- cost infeasibility;
- duplicate identity;
- deterministic dominance;
- insufficient sample or capability.

Every considered and pruned trial is registered for honest selection accounting.

## 5. Abstention and Uncertainty

AI output is not forced to trade. It can abstain because of stale context, novelty, high uncertainty, low expected utility, small candidate margin, excessive cost, portfolio conflict or runtime degradation.

Evaluation includes coverage, selective performance, calibration, missed opportunity and false-positive cost.

## 6. Search Governance

The trainer may not access locked final-test outcomes. Prompts, feature engineering, model selection and analyst feedback are all forms of exposure. Search budgets, objectives and stopping rules are compiled before protected evaluation.

## 7. Knowledge Output

The AI branch stores more than the winning model:

- treatments that consistently failed;
- feature families with no uplift;
- regime-specific strengths and weaknesses;
- cost and delay sensitivity;
- useful model families;
- uncertainty behavior;
- candidate relationships and dominance;
- explanations and proposed falsification experiments.

This knowledge becomes a prior for future contexts without replacing independent validation.
