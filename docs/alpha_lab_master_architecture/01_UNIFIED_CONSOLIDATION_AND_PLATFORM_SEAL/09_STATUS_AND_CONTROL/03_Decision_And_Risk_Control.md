# Decision and Risk Control

## Initial decisions

- The repository itself is Alpha Lab; no nested product directory repeats that name.
- The active consolidation program has seven stages only.
- `src/engine/` is the proposed production source root subject to UC-02 final approval.
- LCM is historical input and is not extended.
- Destructive deletion begins only after preservation, ownership, parity and zero-consumer evidence.

## Initial risks

- dynamic and external consumers may escape static discovery;
- duplicate implementations may hide valid semantic differences;
- documentation mirrors may be mistaken for canonical sources;
- MetaEditor and Strategy Tester evidence depends on the Windows terminal environment;
- very large move waves may destroy bisectability;
- generated and authored files may remain mixed.

Each risk receives an owner and evidence-based closure during UC-01 or UC-02.
