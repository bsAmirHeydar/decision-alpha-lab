# Legacy System Disposition Decisions

## Strategy Factory

V1, V2 and later package generations are not future platform authorities. Their valid capabilities are allocated to the shared engine, Context packages, adapters and tests. Generation names disappear after parity and cutover.

## ACL-OS

Lifecycle, identity, lineage, authority transitions and evidence governance become shared capabilities under kernel, Context, evidence and policy. ACL-OS does not survive as a second operating system.

## UCEE

Treatment, outcomes, research, policy, portfolio economics, runtime and execution capabilities are distributed to their canonical domains. No independent UCEE authority survives.

## SAED

SAED V4 becomes a bounded AI research extension. Unique algorithms survive; duplicated identity, registry, lifecycle, capital and execution primitives do not. V3 is superseded only after differential parity.

## AIEOS

AIEOS becomes engineering standards, policies and tooling. It is not an operating system parallel to Alpha Lab.

## LCM

Reusable verification, hash, recovery and migration utilities move to maintenance tooling. Program-phase machinery becomes historical release material. There is no LCM-17.

## RTHP, NDS and experiments

RTHP becomes the first Golden Context. NDS and experiment families are classified as Contexts, execution adapters, fixtures, bounded examples or history; none own the platform.
