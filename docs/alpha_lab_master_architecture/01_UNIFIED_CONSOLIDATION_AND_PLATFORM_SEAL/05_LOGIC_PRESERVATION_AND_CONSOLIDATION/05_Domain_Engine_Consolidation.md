# Domain Engine Consolidation

## Context and Treatment

One Context model owns occurrence semantics. One Treatment model owns Entry, Stop, Target, Management, expiry, skip and abstention. Context meaning never includes hidden execution policy.

## Research and evidence

Datasets, views, labels, splits, tasks, training, experiments, trial budgets, outcomes, costs, uncertainty, anti-overfit and promotion evidence use shared contracts and ledgers.

## Capital, portfolio and runtime

Evidence creates eligibility, not allocation. Capital and portfolio policies are independently validated. Runtime consumes signed approved bundles and cannot change research definitions.

## Execution and monitoring

Execution adapters translate decisions to external systems through hard risk gates. Monitoring evaluates assumptions, data integrity, runtime health and drift without silently retraining or restoring authority.
