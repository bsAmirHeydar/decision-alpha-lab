# Program Constitution

## Supreme objective

Produce one coherent, maintainable and mechanically usable platform while preserving every material behavior, decision and historical artifact needed for recovery, parity and research memory.

## Precedence

When documents conflict, authority is resolved in this order:

1. accepted architecture constitution and machine policy;
2. versioned contract or schema;
3. accepted ADR;
4. accepted stage charter and exit report;
5. canonical human documentation;
6. generated documentation;
7. historical reference material.

## Mandatory method

Every migration follows: discover → preserve → characterize → assign destination → move or merge → rewrite consumers → prove parity → retire duplicate → record recovery path.

## Non-compensatory gates

No number of passing unit tests compensates for unknown consumers, missing compiled MQL5 evidence, unverified data leakage controls, unresolved authority escalation or absent rollback capability.

## Decision traceability

Every exception requires an ADR or waiver with owner, scope, expiry, risk and removal condition. Verbal decisions and prompts are not architecture authority.

## Termination

The program terminates only at UC-07 with a seal certificate. Remaining work after seal is maintenance, extension or a separately governed architecture change—not continuation of consolidation stages.
