# AI Operating Model

AI is used where it adds conditional selection and pattern discovery while preserving the human-defined market ontology and deterministic execution boundary.

## Three roles

LLMs formalize doctrine, generate adapters/tests, inspect failures, and write reports. Tabular or sequence ML estimates probabilities, expected R, and candidate rankings. Deterministic MQL5/Python engines detect events, enforce risk, and execute.

## Recommended first task

Meta-labeling and policy ranking. The anatomy creates an event; AI decides trade/skip or ranks predeclared candidates. This uses less data, is more explainable, and makes incremental uplift measurable.

## Forbidden authority

AI cannot create hidden features, rewrite event validity, generate undeclared order types, set leverage from confidence alone, train online in the order path, or promote itself.

## Human-in-the-loop transition

Initial human approvals are logged as labels with explicit timestamp and rationale. Models may learn ranking, but human behavior is not assumed optimal. Compare learned decisions with rule baseline and outcome, and retain disagreement datasets.
