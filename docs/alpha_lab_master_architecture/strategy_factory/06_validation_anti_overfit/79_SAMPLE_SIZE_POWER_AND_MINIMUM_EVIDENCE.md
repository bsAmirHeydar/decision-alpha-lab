# Sample Size, Power, and Minimum Evidence

Rows are not evidence unless they represent enough independent market conditions to distinguish signal from noise.

## Evidence units

Report rows, filled candidates, unique events, unique clusters, days, weeks, sessions, symbols, and regimes. Promotion requirements should use unique clusters and effective sample size.

## Power planning

Before confirmation, estimate the smallest economically relevant uplift, expected variance, base rate, and available clusters. Use simulation or bootstrap to estimate the probability of detecting that uplift. If power is low, classify the result as inconclusive rather than negative or positive.

## Sequential research

Optional sequential monitoring requires predeclared boundaries and alpha-spending; repeatedly checking and stopping at a favorable point inflates error. The simpler default is fixed confirmation windows.

## Minimums

No universal number guarantees validity. The manifest may require hundreds of unique clusters, multiple years/regimes, and multiple feeds depending on frequency. Rare convex strategies may have fewer events but need stronger mechanism, longer history, and more conservative capital.
