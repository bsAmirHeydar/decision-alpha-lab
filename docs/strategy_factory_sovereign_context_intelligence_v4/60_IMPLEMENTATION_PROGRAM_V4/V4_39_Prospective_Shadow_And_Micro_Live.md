# Phase V4-39: Prospective Shadow and Micro-Live Qualification

## Mission

Implement the controlled transition from immutable runtime research artifacts into prospective paper and shadow qualification, while engineering—but not self-authorizing—the micro-live control boundary.

## Completed reference slices

1. Immutable V4-38 certificate, handoff and runtime bundle binding.
2. Fifty-two-clause prospective-deployment constitution and non-expanding authority boundary.
3. Frozen prospective cohort, context universe, instrument universe, data roles and observation budget.
4. Closed OFF, PAPER, SHADOW, MICRO_LIVE_QUALIFICATION, MICRO_LIVE and PRODUCTION mode ladder.
5. Intent-only order representation with submission and capital flags permanently false in the reference build.
6. Deterministic paper fill, cost, slippage, rejection and net-outcome ledger.
7. Prospective shadow decision and baseline-disagreement ledger with zero broker side effects.
8. Synthetic demo broker capability and symbol contract.
9. Hard risk envelope covering trade, size, daily loss, drawdown, concurrency, order count, spread, slippage, latency and rejection.
10. Kill-switch, rollback-to-OFF, baseline restoration and incident drill suite.
11. Full intent-to-paper reconciliation with zero live order, fill, position and cash deltas.
12. Telemetry and surveillance for latency, slippage, rejection, disagreement and unauthorized side effects.
13. Closed external-evidence matrix for MetaEditor, Terminal, broker, prospective periods, reconciliation, kill switch, key custody and independent approval.
14. Broker qualification matrix and fail-closed stage gates.
15. Eight-role independent governance, evidence Merkle root, certificate and bounded V4-40 handoff.
16. Closed schemas, examples, mutation tests, MQL5 static mirrors and extensive Obsidian documentation.

## Acceptance decision

Accepted as a synthetic prospective paper and shadow qualification reference. Internal contract, chronology, ledger, reconciliation, risk, surveillance and incident-drill behavior pass. Actual MetaEditor compilation, MT5 Terminal replay, demo/live broker connectivity, prospective paper period, prospective shadow period, live kill-switch drill, key custody and micro-live authorization remain pending external evidence.

## Claim ceiling

This phase does not claim actual prospective success, actual broker qualification, micro-live authorization, capital activation, production authorization or live trading.
