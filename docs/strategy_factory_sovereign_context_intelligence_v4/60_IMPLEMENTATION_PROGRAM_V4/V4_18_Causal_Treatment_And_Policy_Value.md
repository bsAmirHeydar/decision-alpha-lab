# Phase V4-18: Causal Treatment and Policy Value

## Mission

Implement a closed-contract, deterministic and falsifiable causal-treatment and policy-value reference layer over immutable V4-17 mechanism evidence. The phase estimates treatment effects and off-policy value only on a watermarked synthetic benchmark. It preserves Skip and manual baselines and cannot acquire production authority.

## Implemented slices

1. Immutable V4-17 handoff, registry, integrity and claim validation.
2. Frozen finite treatment universe, outcome contract and identification plan.
3. Watermarked multitreatment synthetic benchmark with isolated potential outcomes.
4. Chronological, purged, embargoed and cluster-aware cross-fitting.
5. Propensity, overlap, positivity, clipping and effective-sample-size diagnostics.
6. Difference-in-means, S-learner, T-learner, R-score and AIPW/DR references.
7. ATE, CATE, subgroup summaries and research-only treatment ranking.
8. Skip, fixed, manual, uplift-argmax and conservative policy catalog.
9. Direct, IPW and doubly robust policy value with influence uncertainty.
10. Multiplicity-adjusted lower bounds and lower-tail value.
11. Negative control, propensity, hidden-confounder, cost and transport stresses.
12. Baseline-preserving policy tournament and immutable research checkpoints.
13. Complete compute/exposure ledger, replay, integrity, provenance and V4-19 handoff.
14. Closed schemas, pytest, static MQL5 guards and Obsidian dossier.

## Acceptance state

Local deterministic synthetic reference acceptance is closed. Real-data identification, protected final evaluation, independent external replication, MetaEditor compilation, runtime differential parity, prospective paper, shadow, micro-live and live evidence are not claimed.

## Authority

The phase cannot assert real treatment effects or policy value, rank or select production treatments, allocate risk, sign promotion, compile or activate runtime, access credentials or send orders.
