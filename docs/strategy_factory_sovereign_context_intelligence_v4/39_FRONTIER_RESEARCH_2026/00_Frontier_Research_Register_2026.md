# Purpose

Maintain a source-linked frontier research register for methods that may inform SAED V4. Inclusion is not approval. Every method remains subject to capability tiering, contamination review, baseline comparison, evidence roles, export/runtime feasibility, and UCEE governance.

## Time-series foundation models and representations

- **Foundation Models for Time Series: A Survey** — arXiv:2504.04011. Taxonomy and survey of pretrained time-series models.
- **AdaPTS: Adapting Univariate Foundation Models to Probabilistic Multivariate Time Series Forecasting** — arXiv:2502.10235.
- **Time Series Foundation Models for Multivariate Financial Time Series Forecasting** — arXiv:2507.07296. Useful caution: task-specific models can match or exceed generic foundation models.
- **Aurora: Universal Generative Multimodal Time Series Foundation Model** — arXiv:2509.22295. Research-only intake candidate.
- **RMISC: Large-Scale Real-World Multivariate Corpus** — arXiv:2607.06504. Relevant to multivariate pretraining corpus design.

- **Conformal Risk Control for Nonstationary Portfolio VaR** — arXiv:2602.03903.
- **End-to-End Conformal Calibration for Optimization Under Uncertainty** — arXiv:2409.20534.

## Governance rule

Research-register entries are never cited as proof that a method works for trading. They justify a bounded challenger experiment only. Candidate admission still requires local data, correct estimands, role-safe validation, complete search accounting, independent challenge, prospective evidence, runtime parity, portfolio governance, and operational qualification.
