# Review questions

- Training corpus and contamination must be declared and independently challenged.
- Tokenizer and multivariate support must be declared and independently challenged.
- Probabilistic calibration must be declared and independently challenged.
- Domain adaptation must be declared and independently challenged.
- License and checkpoint supply chain must be declared and independently challenged.
- Economic utility versus specialized baselines must be declared and independently challenged.

## Decision rule

No paper or benchmark result grants production authority. It can only motivate a preregistered governed-challenger experiment.
