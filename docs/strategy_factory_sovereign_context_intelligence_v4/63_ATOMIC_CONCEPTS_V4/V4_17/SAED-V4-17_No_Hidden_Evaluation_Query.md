# SAED V4-17 — No Hidden Evaluation Query

## Definition

**No Hidden Evaluation Query** is an invariant of the SAED V4-17 causal-mechanism discovery boundary. It applies to every variable, environment, graph, equation, score, audit, checkpoint and handoff emitted by this phase.

## Operational consequence

The invariant is enforced through closed schemas, exact registries, deterministic identifiers, known-time rules, graph constraints, authority guards, positive and negative fixtures, mutation tests, replay receipts and content hashes. A violation never becomes a warning-only condition; it resolves to Reject, Quarantine, Abstain or association fallback according to the failure matrix.

## Scientific consequence

All phase evidence is synthetic reference evidence. Even when a candidate graph recovers synthetic benchmark edges, passes negative controls, remains stable across declared environments and yields a bounded orthogonal association score, the result does not establish a real market mechanism or treatment effect. Unmeasured confounding, measurement error, selection, environment misspecification, equivalence classes and transport failure remain unresolved.

## Authority consequence

SAED V4-17 has no causal-claim, treatment-selection, promotion, portfolio, runtime, execution or production authority. The invariant cannot be overridden by a high score, a champion checkpoint, a graph prior, an operator preference or network access. V4-18 receives only immutable research evidence and must perform its own identification and policy-value work.

## Verification

Reviewers reproduce the golden bundle, mutate the relevant invariant, confirm deterministic fail-closed behavior, inspect the exposure ledger for zero protected and hidden-evaluation access, and verify that MQL5 contains static guards only. External evidence remains explicitly separate.
