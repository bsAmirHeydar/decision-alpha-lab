# Support Ratio

## Definition

**Support Ratio** is a closed and explicit concept in SAED V4-41. Its meaning is carried by immutable manifests, versioned policy, known-time observations, deterministic decision traces and content hashes. It is never inferred from mutable process state, operator convention, network reachability or the presence of a deployed artifact.

## Invariants

The concept is deterministic under replay, tenant isolated, namespace scoped, support aware, baseline preserving and reconstructable from evidence. Missing data, stale evidence, detector disagreement, threshold ambiguity, unresolved reconciliation, approval deficiency, dependency uncertainty or authority ambiguity produces abstention, restriction, quarantine, retirement review or escalation. It cannot silently move to a more permissive state.

## Failure modes

Relevant failure classes include future suffixes, incomplete metric/window coverage, unknown fields, duplicate observations, silent policy mutation, cross-tenant actions, detector instability, unresolved incidents, missing replacement, insufficient approval roles, route revocation failure, mutable tombstones, archive deletion, reinstatement without requalification and synthetic evidence misrepresented as external operational truth. Negative and mutation tests must detect these failures.

## Operational boundary

This concept contributes to a deterministic synthetic surveillance and retirement reference. It does not authorize live orders, capital, automatic production actions, broker deployment, real Context retirement or production. Actual external evidence remains separately attached, independently reviewed and signed.

## Relationship

It binds the final V4-41 surveillance policy, observation ledger, detector ensemble, incident lifecycle, containment ladder, dependency graph, retirement state machine, archive, verification, evidence certificate and continuous-operations handoff without mutating central UCEE authority.
