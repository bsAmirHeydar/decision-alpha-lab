# PSI Is a Diagnostic Not Causal Evidence

## Definition
This atomic concept constrains the V4-24 conformal, OOD and selective-control laboratory. It is interpreted inside closed schemas, immutable lineage and known-time research roles. It cannot be reinterpreted as promotion, runtime, risk or execution authority.

## Operational consequence
The implementation exposes the corresponding quantity, assumption, gate or denial in machine-readable evidence. Ambiguous states are prohibited. A failed condition selects the safe `skip` path, records an explicit reason and preserves the baseline. Local overrides are not permitted.

## Scientific consequence
Calibration uncertainty and distribution shift are measured rather than hidden. Marginal coverage, conditional limitations, support, missingness, OOD p-values, coverage and selective risk remain separate observables. Realized outcomes are retrospective evidence and cannot enter a decision artifact. Any claim beyond attached local deterministic evidence is invalid.

## Verification
The concept is tested through positive, negative, mutation and replay cases. Schema closure, content hashes, future-outcome invariance, budget/exposure ledgers and authority-zero checks are mandatory. Static MQL5 evidence is informative only; external compilation and runtime parity remain outside this artifact.
