# Human Search Counts

Human Search Counts is an invariant of the complete-search and exposure-ledger boundary. It exists to prevent silent multiplicity, hindsight contamination, protected-evidence leakage and authority escalation. The invariant is represented in closed contracts, deterministic tests, hash-linked ledgers and the final research-only certificate.

A violation is not treated as missing telemetry. It invalidates the affected ledger scope, blocks the V4-28 handoff and preserves the last accepted baseline. The invariant does not authorize treatment selection, promotion, runtime compilation, risk allocation or execution.
