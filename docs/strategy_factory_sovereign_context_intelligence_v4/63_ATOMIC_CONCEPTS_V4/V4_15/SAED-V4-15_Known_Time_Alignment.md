# Known-Time Alignment

## Definition

Every input is evaluated at one cutoff; information after that cutoff is unavailable.

## Engineering invariants

The concept is closed, versioned, hash-bound, deterministic, known-time safe where temporal semantics apply, and subordinate to the V4-15 authority boundary. It retains source-plane identity, support, missingness, quality, age, and failure semantics. It cannot be interpreted as canonical truth, outcome authority, treatment authority, risk authority, runtime authority, execution authority, economic evidence, or production evidence.

## Failure condition

A missing required identity, unknown field, stale or future-known input, source-hash mismatch, unsupported subset, implicit imputation, corrupt dimension, unrecorded exposure, gate collapse, causal over-interpretation, or authority escalation invalidates the relevant challenger path. The bounded response is baseline, abstain, manual, reject, or quarantine according to the closed support policy.

## Evidence

The reference implementation supplies exact schemas, immutable examples, golden envelopes and masks, exhaustive subset matrices, structured dropout, deterministic candidate outputs, support and fail-closed audits, ablation and permutation diagnostics, replay and integrity receipts, claim ledger, MQL5 static mirrors, and a frozen V4-16 handoff. Synthetic results prove implementation behavior only.
