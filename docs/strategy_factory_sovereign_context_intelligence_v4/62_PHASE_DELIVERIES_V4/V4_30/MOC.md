# SAED V4-30 — Independent And Multi-Lab Replication

This map indexes the complete modular delivery for protocol freeze, package identity, laboratory independence, blinded exchange, preregistration, environment isolation, one-run execution, reconciliation, disagreement control, evidence, QA and the restricted V4-31 handoff.

## Claim ceiling

All reference laboratories and environments are synthetic. External institutional replication, physical separation, real signatures, real alpha, promotion, runtime, risk, execution, production, online learning and live trading remain unclaimed and unauthorized.
