# Evidence Role Immutability

## Purpose

This component defines the **Evidence Role Immutability** control inside the V4-32 multi-agent research constitution. It is content-addressed, deny-by-default, independently reviewable, and subordinate to canonical Context truth and UCEE authority.

## Contract

The control binds declared identities, known-time inputs, immutable evidence roles, typed outputs, budgets, review state, and deterministic hashes. Unknown fields, undeclared permissions, unbound evidence, and non-replayable decisions fail closed.

## Invariants

- No agent receives promotion, runtime, risk-allocation, broker, credential, production, online-learning, or live-trading authority.
- Proposal, implementation, challenge, evidence custody, and approval remain separated where the control is material.
- Protected evidence access is exposure-accounted and requires the declared human checkpoint.
- Dissent, negative results, incidents, and counterexamples cannot be silently removed.
- Exhausted or contradictory paths resolve to quarantine, reject, abstain, manual review, or stop-family.

## Failure handling

Identity mismatch, hash mismatch, future-suffix exposure, self-approval, budget overrun, role collision, source-lineage break, or authority escalation creates a signed incident and automatic containment.

## Authority boundary

This phase may propose, audit, implement, challenge, curate, block, and approve research-only checkpoints. It cannot mutate protected truth, sign promotion, compile or activate runtime, allocate risk, access live credentials, submit orders, authorize production, or trade.

## Acceptance evidence

Acceptance requires closed schemas, deterministic replay, positive and negative tests, mutation coverage, complete prompt/task/output and exposure ledgers, human-review evidence, static MQL5 boundary checks, and an explicit statement that MetaEditor, runtime parity, broker qualification, production authorization, and real alpha are not claimed.
