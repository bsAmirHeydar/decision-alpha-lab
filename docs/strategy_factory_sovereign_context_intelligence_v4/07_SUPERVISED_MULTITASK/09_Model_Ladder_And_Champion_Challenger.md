# Purpose

Define a mandatory complexity ladder and paired champion–challenger process so advanced models earn their place through protected incremental economic value.

# Ladder

1. Skip-All and Always-Trade controls.
2. Unconditional context/treatment tables.
3. Manual policy.
4. Regularized linear/logistic and survival baselines.
5. Calibrated trees and learning-to-rank.
6. Distributional and multi-task models.
7. Sequence, state-space and graph challengers.
8. Foundation adapters and hierarchical meta-learning.
9. Causal challengers.
10. Research-only world and offline-policy models.

# Comparison rule

Every challenger uses identical occurrence clusters, folds, economics, support definitions, calibration role and trial accounting. It must report paired incremental selection, treatment, risk, timing and residual value relative to the current champion.

# Promotion constraints

- Aggregate gain cannot hide critical subgroup loss.
- Complexity cost, latency, export/parity and monitoring burden enter Pareto selection.
- Challenger remains shadow until prospective evidence and UCEE qualification pass.
- Champion is not silently replaced.
- Simple fallback remains deployable.

# Kill criteria

- No reproducible protected uplift.
- Gain disappears after multiplicity or cost stress.
- Support shrinks below operational value.
- Runtime parity or fallback is unavailable.
- Model risk exceeds admitted tier.
