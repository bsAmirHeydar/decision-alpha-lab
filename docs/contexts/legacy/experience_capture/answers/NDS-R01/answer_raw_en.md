## Original Experience, translated to English

Fractality means doing the same thing across different timeframes.

Higher timeframes dominate lower timeframes.

Also include symmetries.

In F-counting, F2 is larger than F1 or at least equal to F1.

In Hook node-counting, Hooks 1, 2, and 3 usually have symmetry.

If Hook 4 happens, Hook 4 may go beyond the symmetry.

This symmetry can help with entry points, zone definition, and improving the accuracy of Extreme entries.
