# BASE-03 — Raw Answer

## Topic

What counts as a reason and what counts as noise.

## Original Experience, translated to English

The main reasons come from the countings.

From the Rally view, the important thing is:

- which F we are in,
- and where exactly we are inside it.

From the Hook view, the important thing is:

- which Hook number we are in.

For example:

- Are we close to the end of the Hook, near 3 or 4?
- Are we inside the second Hook?

When the first Hook is not hit and the market is making the second Hook, it becomes prone to Rally.

In the image, I am showing a Hook-Hook-Rally idea.

That means we look through the windows I mentioned before: multi-scenario and multi-timeframe.

In the end, the state of Hook and Rally, together with the limits of our market anatomy definitions, becomes the framework of our signals.

Outside those frameworks, things are not important at all.

We do not care about candle-based things, technical-analysis games, indicators, and those kinds of things.

Different signals can take different forms.

Examples:

- Hook-Hook-Rally
- When F3 is completed, we zoom into the lower timeframe inside that higher-timeframe F3, and if we see three Fs there, we can take the reversal
- After F1 is formed, if the direction is correct, an entry can be taken with a stop below the flag waist

These are different entry types, and Extreme is only one of them.

I want us to design many entry types and many writable states, and develop all of them, not only Extremes.

There are many ways to exploit this model, because it gives us frameworks and movement constraints from which we can clearly define entry, stop-loss, and target.
