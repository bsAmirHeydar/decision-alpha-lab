# SCN-R04 — Raw Answer

## Topic

Scenario update, constraint integrity, weight change, comparison against new or updated scenarios, repricing, and death.

## Original Experience, translated to English

Each scenario appears because a set of constraints has been respected.

At the same time, each scenario is compared with other scenarios.

It is simple.

We look at how much of its constraints have remained untouched.

Then we look at how its weight has changed compared with newly created scenarios or previously existing scenarios that have been updated.
