# SCN-R03 — Raw Answer

## Topic

Scenario ranking, convex opportunity set, multi-zone selection, potential-first logic, risk/reward openness, and win-rate after convexity.

## Original Experience, translated to English

No scenario necessarily becomes dominant.

We take convex trades based on each scenario's own evidence.

The discussion is about potentials.

We use each one of these threads, not necessarily to make one scenario dominant.

Potential is what matters, not correctness.

The scenario should be lower cost, more profitable, with wider and more open profit potential.

Since win rate is not the main priority, every scenario is continuously tested.

But the preferred scenarios are the ones that are lower cost and more profitable relative to their cost.

After convexity, win rate matters, not before it.
