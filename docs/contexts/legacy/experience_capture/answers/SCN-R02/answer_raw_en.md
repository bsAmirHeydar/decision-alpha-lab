# SCN-R02 — Raw Answer

## Topic

Potential zone definition, context-to-zone promotion, fractal multi-factor analysis, explosive low-cost/high-reward area, lower-timeframe entry refinement, training granularity, and zone invalidation.

## Original Experience, translated to English

A zone is a price range that, based on multi-sided and fractal analysis, is a very good explosion point.

For example, on the higher timeframe we may be in a bearish F2, while on the middle timeframe we may be in a bullish F3. Then we may see which Hook this F3 is completing. The range from the near-death area to the origin node price of that Hook can become our zone.

There are many different combinations through which tradeable zones can be found.

For example, we know F2 is greater than or equal to F1. When price has moved as much as F1, and at the same time it is near the death area of a CycleHook, we can bet on the trade until the midpoint/waist of F2 is eaten. This can also become a zone.

In reality, many combinations exist. We can use any constraint and create a zone.

Context tells us the situation. Zone tells us that this situation is exploitable here.

A zone is a good, high-potential area for entry. After that, the exact entry point is the Extreme on the lower timeframe.

Here, by Extreme I mean entry-level Extreme, not the Hook Extreme. The idea is that the lower-timeframe Hook should close as much as possible, and then we enter with the smallest possible stop.

There is an important point about training layers.

One mode is to train across all markets and all conditions to find the general rules and let the robot learn.

A more detailed mode is to train separately for each market.

A more detailed mode is to train separately for each timeframe in each market.

An even more detailed mode is that even inside that layer, there may not be a stable behavior that is directly exploitable. In that case, we may need to observe a trend in weighting. For example, in one-hour gold, the weight given to Hook may gradually decrease and the weight of F3 as trend-start may increase. This is only an example.

So we should proceed through these layers in order.

A zone becomes invalid when the area itself is destroyed.

Symmetries usually define the start of the zone. The end of the zone can be behind the Hook start.

The final output of a zone should be a price range that indicates a good, high-potential reversal: lower cost and larger explosion.
