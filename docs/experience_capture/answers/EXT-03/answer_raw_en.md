# EXT-03 — Raw Answer

## Topic

Why L2 is the default Extreme anchor level.

## Original Experience, translated to English

L2 is our minimum valid L.

The L concept comes from node logic.

It means at least two candles on the right side and two candles on the left side do not reach that price.

That becomes L2.

L1 is meaningless.

The minimum is L2.

The reason for using it is that we can see and test more Extremes.

We can also take more Extremes.

But there is no dogmatic attachment to 2.

Sometimes, depending on the size of the cycle, increasing this number may be better.

However, in my view, we should try to have the smallest possible stops.
