# EXT-01 — Raw Answer

## Topic

Extreme, untouched node, near-death of cycle, convex entry geometry.

## Original Experience, translated to English

Extreme is one of the best entry models.

But there are other entry models too.

The entry layer must be separated, and we should not look at everything only through this one entry type.

Extreme is one of the entry states, but it can be one of the most logical ones.

It is a very convex entry.

Sometimes the market comes close to the Extreme, but it does not activate the limit order and then reverses.

Sometimes it hits the stop and then reverses.

Because of this, we must deepen our understanding of Extreme and understand the original concept.

Extreme is the concept of being near death.

Near the death of a cycle.

A cycle starts from a node.

When the market moves and comes back to the node and passes it, that is the death of the cycle.

But near death means that price comes close to the death point without necessarily killing it.

That zone is near death.

In my view, the best entries are near death.

There is a special repulsion there.

Of course, not always.

More important than how often reversal happens is that the risk zone is narrow and thin.

That makes it a good risk area.

It can give very good reward.

It can be very convex.

Extreme means taking a trade near the death of a cycle.

The number is variable.

Sometimes it is more, sometimes less.

The 90% number is not special.

The more important question is where we truly want to trade from.

We must understand how much Extreme permission we want to allow.

How narrow can we make the risk zone?

It must not become overfit.

It must not become fragile.

At the same time, we want to create the maximum possible reward through our entries.

These are the important issues.

There is no specific fixed number intended.

Extreme entries can also have many stop-outs.

Sometimes the stop becomes close to the spread size.

These can actually be excellent.

We do not want to filter them out unless the market has a very wide spread overall.

For example, if the raw stop without spread is at least two or three times the spread, that can be acceptable.

Many explosive entries come exactly from these very narrow entries, such as one-minute timeframe Extremes.

The red lines I drew in each image start from a node whose cycle came close to its death Extreme but then reversed.

That is why Extremes are very convex and can give large reward.

The entry is placed near death.

The stop is behind the node.

I am mostly looking for the most extreme entries because the rewards become much more explosive.
