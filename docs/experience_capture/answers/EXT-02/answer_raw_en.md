# EXT-02 — Raw Answer

## Topic

Anchor node source for Extreme and the role of Extreme inside the entry layer.

## Original Experience, translated to English

RTV is not part of NDS at all.

RTV was only a very simple research idea and it has nothing to do with NDS.

So we do not care about RTV in this context.

Extreme is only the entry point.

In reality, through anatomy, multi-layer analysis, and multi-scenario analysis, when we reach a decision for buy or sell inside a certain area, then we go to the lower timeframe and enter using Extremes.

This is how we use it.

In other words, the entry is known.

But the direction and the region for that direction are determined by other parts of the system.

Only after the direction and region are known do we go smaller and take an Extreme.

Now, why did I say L2?

Because a lower level allows you to see and take more Extremes.

A cycle may become very, very large, but the entries can still remain small.

This should be flexible, not too rigid or mechanical.
