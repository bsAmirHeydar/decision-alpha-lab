# SCN-R01 — Raw Answer

## Topic

Context power, position, exploitable constraints, training targets, reversal probability, X/Y closure, symmetry confluence, destination openness, optionality, parent-child dominance, and trade-zone promotion.

## Original Experience, translated to English

The amount of power and even the quality of it must be trained.

In our viewpoint, some things are already clear, and some things are two-state or multi-state. Those ambiguous parts need training.

There are two main places where training is needed.

First: which movement constraint is more exploitable with lower cost? That means lower risk for larger and longer profits.

Second: when there is no single rule and the situation has multiple possible states, which states should we consider so that we pay less cost and make more profit?

Power and reversal probability are important.

Usually at least one X closure is needed for reversal power. Y can define the reversal zone more accurately to some extent, and it shows momentary energy. When both X and Y close, reversal probability is higher.

When several sequences close, power increases.

When symmetry accumulates around a specific area, that area becomes a trading zone, or if we are at the entry level, it becomes the Extreme entry into the trade.

Context means seeing Hook and Rally in bullish and bearish forms. Once the position is understood, and fractally too, good reversal areas can be considered. The probability of reversal is created by the elements of the strategy and anatomy. Then, inside that region, we go to a lower timeframe and take our reversal setup with a limit order.

In addition to structural reasons, there is a concept called open one-and-twos. The market will definitely come with a three and hit them, but it is not known when this will happen. This can be considered for destination.

If destination and take profit are very close, optionality is lower. We must see whether the trade is worth it or not. This comes out from the overall/net view.

It is possible that we reach different entry points and trade each with different risk. Stepwise exits across multiple destinations are also possible. These flexibilities are allowed.

Higher timeframe is dominant. Lower timeframe is used to see micro-structurally how it wants to carry out the higher-timeframe map.

Weak context is based on all the gradings already discussed. All of them must be considered.

In the end, power alone is not important. What matters is the position we are in. After that, what matters is how the constraints of that position behave. The anatomy we described is actually those constraints. We are betting on those constraints.

There are counting constraints, symmetry constraints, Hook constraints, Cycle constraints, Rally constraints. Some levels must not be hit. Some levels must be hit. Some structures have symmetry. All of these must be considered.

So it is not only a matter of power. It is a matter of position in three layers: context, zone, and entry. Then the strength of the constraint that we want to bet on must be assessed.
