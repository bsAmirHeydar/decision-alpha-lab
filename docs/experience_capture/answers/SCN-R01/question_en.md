# SCN-R01 — Context Power Scoring

## Question

What makes one context stronger than another in NDS?

## Why This Question Remains

Context comes before zone and entry. Extreme is not enough by itself. Parent timeframe dominance, Hook type, X/Y closure, multiple sequences, symmetry, destination openness, and tradable constraints may all affect context quality. The system needs a formal model.

## Answer Requirements

Please clarify:

- Is higher timeframe always dominant?
- How do Hook Type A/B/C affect reversal power?
- How do X closure and Y closure affect reversal probability and zone precision?
- Does multiple sequence closure increase context power?
- How does symmetry confluence create a tradable zone or entry Extreme?
- When does context become a trade zone?
- How does destination openness affect optionality?
- How should parent and child timeframe conflict be handled?
- What makes context weak?
- Should final output be a score, class, state, or policy decision?
