# BASE-04 — Which Concepts Must Never Enter the System?

## Question

Which concepts do you absolutely not want to enter the system?

## Why This Matters

To prevent ontology contamination, the forbidden concepts must be explicit.

## Required Clarifications

Please clarify:

- Are classical indicators forbidden?
- Are time-of-day, day-of-week, or artificial time-horizon concepts forbidden?
- Should ICT/classical concepts be removed unless they are translated into our own language?

## Image Requirement

No image is required.

A text answer is enough unless you have a visual example.
