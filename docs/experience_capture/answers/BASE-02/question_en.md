# BASE-02 — What Exactly Is a Scenario?

## Question

From your perspective, what exactly is a scenario, and what must it contain in order to count as a scenario?

## Why This Matters

Later, we need to convert scenarios into a dataset.

Therefore, we must know whether a scenario is only a directional idea or a complete case file.

## Required Clarifications

Please clarify:

- Does a scenario need entry, stop, destination, and reasons?
- What is the difference between a weak scenario and a dead scenario?
- Can a scenario be alive without an entry, but only with a directional thesis?
