# SCN-R02 — Potential Zone Definition

## Question

What turns a context or viewpoint into a Potential Zone / Trading Zone in NDS?

## Why This Question Remains

Context identifies structural position. Zone identifies the region where that position becomes exploitable. Entry is then refined inside the zone on a lower scale. The system needs a formal model for context-to-zone promotion.

## Answer Requirements

Please clarify:

- What is a zone?
- What sources can create a zone?
- What is the difference between context and zone?
- Where is a reversal zone built?
- How does zone relate to lower-timeframe Extreme entry?
- How does symmetry create zone boundaries?
- How does destination/optionality affect zone value?
- Can multiple zones exist at the same time?
- What invalidates or expires a zone?
- What should the final algorithmic output of a zone be?
