# EXT-09 — Raw Answer

## Topic

Anchor-node quality, Extreme quality, context priority, reward-first expectancy, and layered training.

## Original Experience, translated to English

The quality of the Extremes themselves is not important.

They are only entry points.

The main work is understanding the context and finding a zone that has potential.

The main thing is potential, not win rate.

Our criterion is positive expectancy and the ability to capture good potentials.

The main story is that the first priority is reward and being exposed to explosive points.

After that comes win rate.

But regarding these Extremes themselves, train on them and see.

Maybe there is a rule.

In general, I want training to work like this:

The model should tell me what it has learned.

Then I look at it and give my opinion.

After that, we run tests.

The training itself should be layered and branch-by-branch.

We should train with different algorithms and see what happens.
