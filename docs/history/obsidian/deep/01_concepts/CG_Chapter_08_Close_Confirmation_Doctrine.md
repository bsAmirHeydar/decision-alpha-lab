# CG Chapter 08 — Close Confirmation Doctrine

## Core Idea

Chapter 08 defines the strategic boundary between a raw hunt and a confirmed divergence.

A hunt can occur inside an unfinished candle. But the divergence only becomes valid after the active candle closes and the asymmetry still exists.

## Doctrine

> The hunt is discovered through high/low behavior, but divergence is believed only after candle close.

## Why This Matters

Without this doctrine, the strategy would react to incomplete market behavior. An open candle can still change the relationship between the two markets. The clean symbol can still hunt. The apparent asymmetry can disappear.

Candle close protects the strategy from accepting an unfinished relationship.
