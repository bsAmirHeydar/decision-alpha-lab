# FP-I06 Raw Candidate Is Not A Confirmed Signal

## Definition

A raw candidate records persistent M1 asymmetry and waits for FP-I07 host-candle confirmation. It has no execution authority.

## Operational consequence

- Encode the concept in immutable contracts.
- Include behavior-bearing inputs in semantic identity.
- Preserve negative and ambiguous outcomes as evidence.
- Test deterministic replay and changed-input divergence.
