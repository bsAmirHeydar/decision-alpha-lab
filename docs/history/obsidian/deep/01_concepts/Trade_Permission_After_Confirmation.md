# Trade Permission After Confirmation

## Core Idea

A confirmed divergence creates permission to trade.

## Meaning

When the candle closes and the divergence remains valid, the strategy has the right to treat the event as actionable.

## Important Distinction

Trade permission does not mean the final risk system must allocate capital to every signal. It means the signal is strategically valid.

## Base Law

```text
Confirmed divergence = trade permission
```

## Future Layers

Later rules may decide:

- maximum simultaneous signals,
- risk allocation,
- conflict handling,
- portfolio exposure,
- statistical filtering.

But these do not change the base signal permission.
