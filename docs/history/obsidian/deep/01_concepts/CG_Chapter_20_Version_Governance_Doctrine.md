# CG Chapter 20 — Version Governance Doctrine

Chapter 20 protects the current EXP0017 strategy from premature change.

The current model is not allowed to change strategy rules. It may only collect statistics, compare families, rank outcomes, identify weaknesses, and prepare future recommendations.

## Core law

The current strategy remains fixed until the strategy architect explicitly opens a future optimization version.
