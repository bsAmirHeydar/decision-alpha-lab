# Complete Search Universe Is Statistical Evidence

The statistical denominator is the complete declared search universe, not the surviving winners. Failed, pruned, skipped, invalid, cancelled, timed-out, selected, ensembled, and overridden choices all remain visible.

## Operational consequence

This concept is enforced by executable contracts, negative fixtures, closed schemas, and the non-compensatory promotion gate. A consumer may not weaken it through undocumented interpretation.
