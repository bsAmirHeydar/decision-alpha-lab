# No Base Position Limit Doctrine

The base model has no position limit.

## No limits on

- total trades;
- open trades;
- same-CG trades;
- same-symbol trades;
- same-direction trades;
- opposite-direction trades;
- trades after loss;
- trades after win.

## Future possibility

Limits may be introduced later if statistics show that they improve expectancy, reduce drawdown, or reduce stop streaks without damaging signal quality.
