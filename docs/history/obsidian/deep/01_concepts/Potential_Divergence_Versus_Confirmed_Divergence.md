# Potential Divergence Versus Confirmed Divergence

## Potential Divergence

A potential divergence exists when one symbol has hunted a reference and the other symbol has not, but the active candle is still open.

It is not yet trade permission.

## Confirmed Divergence

A confirmed divergence exists after the active candle closes and the clean symbol still has not hunted.

## Lifecycle

```text
Reference exists
↓
One-sided hunt appears
↓
Potential divergence
↓
Candle closes
↓
Asymmetry survives
↓
Confirmed divergence
```

## Failure Path

```text
Reference exists
↓
One-sided hunt appears
↓
Potential divergence
↓
Clean symbol also hunts
↓
Divergence invalid
```
