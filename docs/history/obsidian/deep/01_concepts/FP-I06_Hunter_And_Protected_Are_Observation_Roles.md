# FP-I06 Hunter And Protected Are Observation Roles

## Definition

Hunter is the touching symbol and Protected is the preserving symbol. These roles do not by themselves select the trade symbol.

## Operational consequence

- Encode the concept in immutable contracts.
- Include behavior-bearing inputs in semantic identity.
- Preserve negative and ambiguous outcomes as evidence.
- Test deterministic replay and changed-input divergence.
