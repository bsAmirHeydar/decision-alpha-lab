# All Confirmed Divergences Are Visible

## Core Doctrine

All confirmed divergences should be displayed and preserved.

## Reason

The strategy must not erase data before statistical discovery.

## Implications

Display all confirmed divergences across:

- all cycle groups,
- both directions,
- both symbols,
- all same-day references,
- cash and outside-cash conditions,
- near and far reference families.

## Why It Matters

Visibility enables:

- unbiased review,
- signal clustering analysis,
- conflict observation,
- later ranking,
- model building,
- human learning.
