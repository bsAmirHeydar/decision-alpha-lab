## Summary

NDS must distinguish two different Extreme concepts: They are not the same object. The Hook Extreme is an internal structural extreme of a Hook/CycleHook. The Entry-Level Extreme is an execution-refinement condition where the entry-scale CycleHook is extremely close to death, creating a small stop and a more convex trade. Recommended canonical term: A Hook Extreme is the opposite price extreme inside a Hook/CycleHook. For a positive Hook that starts from a valley: In more general terms: This belongs to the structure of the Hook/CycleHook itself. It is not automatically an entry trigger. An Entry-Level Extreme means: This is the practical execution concept. It is a near-death condition at the entry level.
