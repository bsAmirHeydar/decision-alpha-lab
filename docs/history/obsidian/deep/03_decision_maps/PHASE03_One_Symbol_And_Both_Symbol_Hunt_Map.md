# Phase 03 One-Symbol and Both-Symbol Hunt Map

```text
A high hunted, B high not hunted -> one-symbol high hunt raw state
B high hunted, A high not hunted -> one-symbol high hunt raw state
A low hunted, B low not hunted   -> one-symbol low hunt raw state
B low hunted, A low not hunted   -> one-symbol low hunt raw state
A and B high hunted              -> both-symbol high hunt raw state
A and B low hunted               -> both-symbol low hunt raw state
```

The map does not assign divergence, invalidation, entry, or ranking.
