## خلاصه

This repository documents an ongoing investigation into the structural nature of financial markets. The central hypothesis is that markets possess structural memory rather than fixed temporal memory, and that transitions between momentum and mean-reverting regimes can be inferred from decision nodes and their associated energy. Whether the hypothesis is ultimately validated or rejected, the objective is to produce transparent, reproducible research.
