## خلاصه

Draft H0001 proposed that structurally defined highs and lows may represent privileged regions of market interaction rather than arbitrary price locations. However, H0001 did not specify how such structural locations should be revisited and evaluated after their initial formation. A common assumption among discretionary traders is that previously established structural highs and lows continue to influence market behavior when revisited. These locations are often described as areas where the market "remembers" prior interactions. This assumption remains largely anecdotal.
