## خلاصه

Extreme, untouched node, near-death of cycle, convex entry geometry. Extreme is one of the best entry models. But there are other entry models too. The entry layer must be separated, and we should not look at everything only through this one entry type. Extreme is one of the entry states, but it can be one of the most logical ones. It is a very convex entry. Sometimes the market comes close to the Extreme, but it does not activate the limit order and then reverses. Sometimes it hits the stop and then reverses.
