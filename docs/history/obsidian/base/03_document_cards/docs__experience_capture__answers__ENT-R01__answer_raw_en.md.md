## خلاصه

Entry-level Extreme, Hook Extreme distinction, Extreme Near Death, small stop, and convex exploitation. Look, Hook Extreme means this: For example, a Hook starts from a valley. From that valley to the highest price it sees before the touch, that highest price is the Hook Extreme. Entry Extreme is different. What I mean by Entry Extreme is that the cycle has become excessively close to death. In other words: The purpose is to get a smaller stop and a more convex exploitation.
