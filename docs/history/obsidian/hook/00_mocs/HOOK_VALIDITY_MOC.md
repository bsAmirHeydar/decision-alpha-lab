# Hook Validity MOC

## Central Thesis

Not every hook is valid. Because hooks are fractal, unrestricted hook counting creates noise. Alpha Lab accepts only two valid hook families for zone generation:

1. [[../01_concepts/Hook_After_Hook|Hook After Hook]]
2. [[../01_concepts/Hook_After_Opposing_F3|Hook After Opposing F3]]

Everything else is [[../01_concepts/Invalid_Fractal_Hook|Invalid Fractal Hook]] unless proven otherwise through later research.
