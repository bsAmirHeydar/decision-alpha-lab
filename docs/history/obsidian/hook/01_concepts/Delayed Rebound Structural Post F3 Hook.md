# Delayed Rebound Structural Post F3 Hook

A delayed/rebound structural post-F3 Hook forms after price reaches the terminal side of F3, rebounds, and then creates a complete structural Hook slightly away from the terminal endpoint.

Canonical requirements:

- F3 is completed or locked.
- Hook direction opposes F3 direction.
- Hook origin is after the F3 terminal time.
- Hook origin is inside the post-F3 ownership window.
- Hook is not classified as direct.
- Hook has a full structural sequence.

Chart label: `F3H-R-S`.
