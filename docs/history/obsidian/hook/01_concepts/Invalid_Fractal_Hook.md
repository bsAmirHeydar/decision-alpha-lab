# Invalid Fractal Hook

An Invalid Fractal Hook is any hook-like formation that does not qualify as:

1. Hook After Hook, or
2. Hook After Opposing F3.

Invalid hooks may be visible, but they are not reliable primary zone sources.

## Policy

> The system should prefer missing a hook over trusting structurally unstable hook noise.
