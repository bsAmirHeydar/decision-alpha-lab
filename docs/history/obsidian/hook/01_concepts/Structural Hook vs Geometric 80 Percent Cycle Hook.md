# Structural Hook vs Geometric 80 Percent Cycle Hook

## Structural Hook

A Structural Hook has complete Hook anatomy:

- structural origin node;
- sequence nodes;
- crown/opposite extreme;
- confirmed terminal node;
- closed lifecycle state.

This is the clean production-grade Hook.

## Geometric 80% Cycle Hook

A Geometric 80% Cycle Hook is a cycle/semicircle candidate that reaches near-death completion but may not have full structural sequence labels.

It is useful for research and for recognizing the user's intended post-F3 Hook when the chart forms a small cycle after rebound.

## Production policy

Production can run in either:

```text
STRUCTURAL_ONLY
STRUCTURAL_OR_GEOMETRIC_80
```

The mode must be explicit.
