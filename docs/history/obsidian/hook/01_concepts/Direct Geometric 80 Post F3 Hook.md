# Direct Geometric 80 Post F3 Hook

A direct geometric post-F3 Hook starts near the F3 terminal endpoint but does not satisfy the full structural sequence standard. It is still useful when its cycle geometry has completed at least 80% toward death.

Canonical requirements:

- F3 is completed or locked.
- Hook direction opposes F3 direction.
- Hook origin is near the F3 terminal endpoint.
- Hook has a valid crown.
- Hook completion percentage is at least `InpHookPostF3GeometricMinCompletionPct`.

Chart label: `F3H-D80`.
