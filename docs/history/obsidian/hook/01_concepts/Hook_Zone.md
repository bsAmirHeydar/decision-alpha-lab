# Hook Zone

A Hook Zone is a zone derived from a valid hook.

A valid hook zone must have:

- valid hook source,
- entry side,
- stop/expiration side,
- acceptable width,
- open reward potential,
- context-based reason for reaction or reversal.

## Rule

> Hook validity comes before hook-zone validity.
