# Direct Structural Post F3 Hook

A direct structural post-F3 Hook starts from the terminal side of an opposing F3 and owns a complete structural Hook sequence.

Canonical requirements:

- F3 is completed or locked.
- Hook direction opposes F3 direction.
- Hook origin is near the F3 terminal endpoint by time and price tolerance.
- Hook terminal is confirmed.
- Hook has a valid crown.
- Hook has at least two same-side sequence nodes.

Chart label: `F3H-D-S`.
