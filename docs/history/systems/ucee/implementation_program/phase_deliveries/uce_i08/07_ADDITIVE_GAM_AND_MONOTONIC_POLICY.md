# Additive, Spline, and Monotonic Policy

Spline-logistic and spline-ridge pipelines provide nonlinear but additive references. They separate smooth univariate effects from interaction-dependent edge. Spline bases and downstream linear models are fit inside each training fold; leakage through globally fitted knots or scaling is forbidden.

Partial dependence and accumulated-local-effect artifacts are audit tools. They record feature grid, row role, fold, model state, dataset manifest, and whether the artifact was eligible for selection.

Hierarchical shrinkage and richer constrained GAM adapters remain extension points. They may not be represented as implemented merely because a descriptor or documentation placeholder exists.
