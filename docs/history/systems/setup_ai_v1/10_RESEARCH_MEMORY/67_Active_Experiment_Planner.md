# Active Experiment Planner

## Objective

Rank future experiments by expected information and economic value, not by likelihood of producing an attractive backtest.

## Inputs

Uncertainty, portfolio gaps, failed hypotheses, redundant trials, sample availability, cost, compute/human effort, reuse potential and false-discovery risk.

## Candidate Experiments

- collect more occurrences for weakly supported profile;
- distinguish Market versus Limit with paper/shadow counterfactuals;
- test a matched null;
- stress a fragile trail;
- validate cross-symbol transfer;
- retire a low-value branch.

All proposed experiments still pass the Experiment Compiler and firewall.
