# Model Ladder and Complexity Budget

## Required Ladder

1. Skip all.
2. Always trade one fixed Treatment.
3. Unconditional treatment means/base rates.
4. Session/time-only.
5. Context-only scorecards.
6. Manual policy.
7. Regularized logistic/ridge.
8. Shallow trees/boosting.
9. Rankers and survival/distributional models.
10. Multi-task, sequence, graph and ensemble challengers.
11. Bounded offline policy learning.

## Permission Rule

A complex model must earn protected, stable economic uplift over the strongest simpler baseline, not only a small predictive metric improvement.

## Complexity Costs

Include compute, latency, calibration fragility, explainability, parity, export risk, maintenance and sample demand in model-risk scoring.
