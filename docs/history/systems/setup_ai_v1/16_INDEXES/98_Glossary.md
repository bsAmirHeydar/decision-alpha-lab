# Glossary

- **Context:** known-time semantic market state.
- **Setup Archetype:** falsifiable exploitation mechanism family.
- **Payoff Profile:** desired economic distribution and constraints.
- **Entry Mechanism:** activation method and its premium/cost.
- **Treatment Atom:** one versioned executable behavior.
- **Treatment Bundle:** complete entry/stop/exit/management action.
- **Opportunity Cluster:** one Context occurrence and all siblings.
- **Outcome Cube:** candidate outcomes on executable market paths.
- **Trainer Task:** bounded predictive/ranking/survival problem.
- **Experiment Lineage:** complete frozen selection process.
- **Promotion Dossier:** evidence used by deterministic gate.
- **Policy Graph:** bounded manual/AI/hybrid decision program.
- **Abstention:** explicit refusal due to support/uncertainty/utility.
- **Fallback:** certified lower-authority alternative.
- **Edge Genome:** reusable profile of validated behavior and risks.
