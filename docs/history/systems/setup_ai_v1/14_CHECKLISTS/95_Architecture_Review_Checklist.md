# Checklist — Architecture and Research Readiness

- [ ] Context semantics and known time are frozen.
- [ ] Setup archetypes are falsifiable and separate from outcomes.
- [ ] Payoff, entry, trigger, stop, exit and management axes are explicit.
- [ ] Compatibility graph prevents incoherent combinations.
- [ ] Skip and Manual baseline are included.
- [ ] Candidate universe and all pruning reasons are frozen.
- [ ] Outcome engine is side-aware and path-accurate where required.
- [ ] Non-fill and censored rows are retained correctly.
- [ ] Siblings remain clustered in folds and resampling.
- [ ] Data roles, purge, embargo and final-test firewall are technical.
- [ ] Model ladder begins with transparent baselines.
- [ ] OOF predictions are used for stacked selectors/calibration.
- [ ] Trial budgets and stopping rules are predeclared.
- [ ] Nulls, stresses and profile-specific anti-overfit tests exist.
- [ ] Promotion blockers are conjunctive and deterministic.
- [ ] Policy support, abstention and fallback are explicit.
- [ ] Runtime preprocessing/model parity is testable.
- [ ] Monitoring assumptions and retirement actions are defined.
