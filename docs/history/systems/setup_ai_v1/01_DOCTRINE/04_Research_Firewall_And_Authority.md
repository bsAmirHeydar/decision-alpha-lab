# Research Firewall and Authority Boundary

## Protected Roles

```text
Exploration → Training → Calibration → Selection Validation → Locked Final Test
→ Prospective Paper → Micro-Live → Live
```

Any observation of a protected role by a human, script, LLM, dashboard or report is an exposure. If the exposure changes feature design, search, thresholds or policy, that role is consumed for the lineage.

## Authority

- Trainer proposes predictions and candidates.
- Experiment engine records all attempts.
- Promotion gate decides evidence state.
- Policy graph decides bounded action/fallback.
- Risk/Portfolio may veto.
- Runtime executes only signed artifacts.

No AI narrative, impressive chart or high Sharpe can override leakage, integrity, parity or safety blockers.
