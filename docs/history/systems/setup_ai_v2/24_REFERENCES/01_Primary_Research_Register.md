## Institutional caution

Generic time-series forecasting performance is not equivalent to exploitable, cost-adjusted, capacity-aware trading edge. Foundation models and deep sequence architectures must remain challengers until they demonstrate incremental economic value against manual, linear, tree, survival, and ranking baselines under locked prospective evidence.
