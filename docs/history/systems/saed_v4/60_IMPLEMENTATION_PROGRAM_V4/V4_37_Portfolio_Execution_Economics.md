# Phase V4-37: Portfolio Execution Economics

## Mission

Implement the deterministic portfolio and execution-economics layer that translates frozen research opportunities into auditable net-edge, capacity, allocation, risk, scheduling, stress and reconciliation evidence while preserving UCEE and denying live authority.

## Completed engineering slices

1. Immutable V4-36 certificate and handoff binding.
2. Twenty-four-clause economics constitution and explicit authority boundary.
3. Instrument master with tick, multiplier, lot, currency, margin and shortability contracts.
4. Known-time FX snapshot with deterministic direct, inverse and cross conversion.
5. Commission, exchange, regulatory, tax, borrow, funding and minimum-fee accounting.
6. Liquidity profiles with ADV, depth, spread, participation cap, session and volume curve.
7. Synthetic square-root temporary/permanent impact with depth penalty and uncertainty band.
8. Correlation, volatility, covariance, shrinkage and cluster dependence evidence.
9. Capital, cash, gross, net, instrument, opportunity, cluster, risk, cost and edge constraints.
10. Opportunity capacity surface and binding-limit attribution.
11. Break-even, expected and conservative net economics for every opportunity.
12. Deterministic constrained allocation, risk scaling and rejection ledger.
13. Portfolio snapshot with signed exposure and dependence-aware risk.
14. Volume-curve non-executable order slicing with no broker route.
15. Six-dimensional stress suite for cost, liquidity, dependence, funding, fill and edge decay.
16. Synthetic capital reservation ledger and synthetic fill reconciliation.
17. Four-role governance, independent challenge and plan-hash-bound approval.
18. Baseline preservation, evidence bundle, certificate and bounded V4-38 handoff.
19. Closed schemas, fixtures, tests, MQL5 static mirrors and Obsidian corpus.

## Acceptance gates

- Zero unknown fields in closed contracts.
- No future-suffix sensitivity.
- Deterministic identity, allocation and replay.
- Full explicit and implicit cost accounting.
- Bounded participation, depth and portfolio exposure.
- Cash reserve and baseline preservation.
- Fail-closed economics, stress and governance gates.
- UCEE compatibility and no central-engine mutation.
- External MetaEditor, terminal, broker and capital evidence remain explicitly separate.

## Claim ceiling

The accepted implementation is a synthetic deterministic portfolio-execution-economics reference. It does not claim real calibrated market impact, broker fills, prospective execution quality, runtime parity, production authorization or live trading.
