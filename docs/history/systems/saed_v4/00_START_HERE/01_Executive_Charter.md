## Mission

Build an institution-grade learner that discovers and validates context-dependent setup edges without granting research models execution authority.

## Non-negotiable principles

- Context semantics are inherited from UCEE and never redefined by the trainer.
- Skip and abstain are first-class actions.
- All treatment candidates are compiler-approved and finite.
- Economic truth uses executable bid/ask paths and realistic costs.
- Complexity must earn incremental value against simpler baselines.
- No research exposure may silently contaminate a locked test.
- Promotion is a governance action, never a model action.

## Canonical interfaces

- `ContextPackage@exact_version`
- `ContextOccurrence`
- `TreatmentUniverseManifest`
- `OutcomeCubeManifest`
- `TrainerProgramManifest`
- `PromotionDossier`
- `RuntimeHandoffBundle`

## Failure modes that must be designed out

- Pattern mining that invents mutable context definitions.
- Winner-only trial retention.
- Using simulated fill as actual fill evidence.
- Optimizing one global metric across incompatible payoff profiles.
- Allowing foundation models or agents to act as unsupported oracles.
- Treating backtest performance as production qualification.

## Evidence and acceptance gates

- Complete traceability from context occurrence to decision.
- Manual, naive, classical, and advanced baselines on identical folds.
- Nested purged walk-forward and untouched prospective challenge.
- Model-risk committee approval with signed dossier.
- Exact runtime and MQL5 parity before activation.

## UCEE compatibility

SAED consumes UCEE I01–I10 context, feature, label, and dataset contracts; uses I11–I12 training and promotion primitives; emits I13 policy graphs; compiles through I14; runs tournaments under I15; onboards through I16; allocates through I17; and qualifies through I18.

## Authority boundary

Research and model components may propose, rank, simulate, or abstain. They may not sign promotion, mutate an active runtime generation, bypass the hard risk gate, reserve portfolio risk without the portfolio engine, or submit broker orders.

## Required outputs

Every run must emit immutable manifests, exact data-role lineage, complete candidate and trial ledgers, calibrated uncertainty, economic assumptions, failure evidence, and a machine-readable handoff.
