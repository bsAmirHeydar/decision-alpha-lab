# SAED V4-23 Atomic — Online Learning Is Forbidden

## Statement
**Online Learning Is Forbidden.** This statement is an invariant of the offline-policy research boundary, not an implementation preference.

## Meaning
The V4-23 laboratory operates only on immutable, known-time, role-declared logged replay and V4-22 synthetic stress evidence. Candidate policies are constrained by observed behavior support, explicit masks, safe projections and the manual baseline. Any interpretation that converts this atomic statement into promotion, runtime, portfolio or execution authority is invalid.

## Engineering consequence
Schemas remain closed; identities and hashes remain deterministic; trial, exposure and compute budgets remain complete; failures remain explicit. The reference implementation must preserve Skip, reject unsupported actions, report estimator disagreement and produce replayable artifacts.

## Scientific consequence
Offline estimates describe bounded research evidence under assumptions. They do not erase confounding, support shift, reward misspecification, model approximation or importance-weight instability. Synthetic paths contribute only negative stress evidence. Uncertainty and abstention are first-class outputs.

## Verification
The invariant is covered by contract tests, mutation tests, golden replay, authority checks, baseline-preservation checks and the V4-23 certificate. Actual MetaEditor, runtime parity, prospective shadow and production evidence are not claimed.
