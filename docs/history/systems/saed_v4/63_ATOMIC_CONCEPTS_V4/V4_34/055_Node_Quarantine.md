# SAED V4-34 Atomic 055 — Node Quarantine

**Definition.** Node Quarantine is treated as a first-class, immutable research contract rather than an implicit operational assumption.

**Invariant.** It must remain deterministic, lineage-complete, known-time safe, budgeted, reviewable and incapable of granting trading or production authority.

**Failure action.** Reject, quarantine, abstain or preserve the baseline. Silent degradation is forbidden.

**Verification.** Closed-schema validation, negative mutation, exact replay and evidence-hash comparison are mandatory. External runtime claims remain unavailable until attached evidence exists.
