# Domain-Shift Card

## Definition

Support must be explicit through shift, scale, missingness, routing concentration, and token-count diagnostics.

## Invariants

The concept is closed, hash-bound, known-time safe where temporal semantics apply, and subordinate to the V4-14 authority boundary. It cannot be interpreted as outcome authority, treatment authority, risk authority, runtime authority, or production evidence.

## Failure condition

A missing identity, mutable upstream reference, unknown field, future dependency, unrecorded external dependency, unsupported input, or authority escalation invalidates the artifact and routes it to quarantine, baseline fallback, or abstention.

## Evidence

The reference implementation supplies machine-readable contracts, deterministic artifacts, mutation tests, replay evidence, claim ledger, and frozen handoff evidence for this concept.
