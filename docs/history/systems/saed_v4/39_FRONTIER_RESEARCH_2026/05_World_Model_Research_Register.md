# Review questions

- Realism must be declared and independently challenged.
- Uncertainty must be declared and independently challenged.
- Intervention semantics must be declared and independently challenged.
- Simulator exploitation must be declared and independently challenged.
- Synthetic evidence classification must be declared and independently challenged.
- Rare-event coverage must be declared and independently challenged.

## Decision rule

No paper or benchmark result grants production authority. It can only motivate a preregistered governed-challenger experiment.
