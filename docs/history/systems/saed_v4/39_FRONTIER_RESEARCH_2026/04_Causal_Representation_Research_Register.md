# Review questions

- Identifiability assumptions must be declared and independently challenged.
- Instantaneous relations must be declared and independently challenged.
- Interventions must be declared and independently challenged.
- Negative controls must be declared and independently challenged.
- Latent confounding must be declared and independently challenged.
- Transport must be declared and independently challenged.

## Decision rule

No paper or benchmark result grants production authority. It can only motivate a preregistered governed-challenger experiment.
