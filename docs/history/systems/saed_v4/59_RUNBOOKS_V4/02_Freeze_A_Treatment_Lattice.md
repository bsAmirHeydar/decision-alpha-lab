# Purpose

This runbook governs **Freeze A Treatment Lattice** as a controlled institutional process.

## Entry conditions

- Named owner, independent reviewer and model-risk owner.
- Immutable Context, treatment, data-role and environment manifests.
- Declared hypothesis family, compute budget and exposure budget.
- UCEE authority boundaries acknowledged.
- Stop conditions and incident channels configured.

## Procedure

1. Validate hashes, schemas, evidence roles and known-time lineage.
2. Reproduce deterministic baselines and golden fixtures.
3. Execute the approved research or operational slice inside the declared sandbox.
4. Record every trial, query, chart, prompt, manual intervention and failed run.
5. Run leakage, mutation, support, calibration and transport diagnostics.
6. Apply cost, delay, capacity, tail and path stresses.
7. Compare against simpler baselines and declared nulls.
8. Freeze the candidate before hidden or independent evaluation.
9. Produce evidence, model-risk, security and limitation dossiers.
10. Route to UCEE admission, redesign, quarantine, retirement or rejection.

## Stop conditions

- Future information or role contamination.
- Missing or inconsistent lineage.
- Search/exposure ledger gap.
- Critical OOD/support failure.
- Material calibration failure.
- Unresolved execution infeasibility.
- Red-team critical finding.
- Hidden-evaluation query budget exhausted.
- Runtime parity mismatch.

## Mandatory outputs

- Signed run manifest.
- Complete trial and exposure ledger.
- Baseline and challenger scorecards.
- Failure and incident ledger.
- Support/calibration/uncertainty report.
- Cost/capacity/transport report.
- Independent review decision.
- Explicit unresolved limitations.

## Completion gate

Completion means the process is reproducible and governed. It does not mean alpha, promotion or production qualification unless the respective UCEE and prospective gates also pass.
