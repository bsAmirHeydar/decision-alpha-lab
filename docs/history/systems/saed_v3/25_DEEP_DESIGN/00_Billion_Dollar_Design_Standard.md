# Objective

Define institutional quality for Alpha Lab and prevent advanced AI from becoming an excuse for weak evidence, hidden leverage or ungoverned complexity.

## Capability tier

**Core Production**

# Non-negotiable principles

- Profit is an outcome; the system optimizes repeatable scientific discovery, capital protection and controlled compounding.
- Every decision is reproducible from immutable data, code, environment, context, treatment, model, calibration and policy hashes.
- Every advanced model competes against manual, naive, regularized and economically simple baselines on identical folds and costs.
- No research component receives order, broker, capital-transfer, signature, promotion or protected-data authority by default.
- Capacity, impact, liquidity, concentration and operational resilience are part of edge validity.
- Negative evidence, failed trials, incidents and retirements are permanent first-class assets.

# Reference architecture

- Three planes: immutable evidence, governed research/control, bounded production decision.
- Content-addressed artifacts and signed manifests reconstruct the exact causal chain of every result.
- Finite versioned action lattices let models select valid treatments and Skip/Abstain rather than invent live actions.
- Independent model-risk and statistical-adversary functions can block but cannot manufacture evidence.
- Authority progresses through research-only, challenger, shadow, restricted production and qualified production.

# Algorithms and decision logic

- Compute value after spread, slippage, commission, financing, adverse selection, capacity haircuts and portfolio marginal-risk cost.
- Rank with lower-confidence-bound utility and reject unstable tails or narrow parameter islands.
- Require prospective evidence for every promoted edge and independent evidence for any material increase in risk, scope or complexity.

# Data, role and lineage controls

1. Raw, canonical, feature, outcome, training, calibration, selection-validation, locked-final, prospective, shadow and live roles are distinct.
2. Human, model, agent, dashboard and report access to protected evidence is recorded in the exposure ledger.
3. Revisions create a new artifact and never rewrite decision-time evidence.
4. Cross-fold preprocessing, tokenization, graph construction, feature selection and model selection are prohibited.
5. Trial, candidate, failure, retry, prune, timeout and waiver records remain immutable.

# Measurement system

- Evidence reproducibility
- Protected-data exposure
- Trial-universe completeness
- Capacity-adjusted utility
- Incident containment time
- Rollback time
- Model concentration risk
- Research cost per validated edge

Metrics are segmented by time, symbol, context, regime, payoff profile, entry mechanism, broker/economics profile, support bucket and evidence class. Aggregates may not hide unstable subgroups.

# Adversarial failure table

| Class | Failure mechanism |
|---|---|
| Failure | Backtest success is presented as production readiness. |
| Failure | Complexity is rewarded without incremental economic value. |
| Failure | One model, feed, broker, symbol or regime becomes a hidden single point of failure. |
| Failure | Synthetic or simulated evidence is mislabeled actual. |

# Authority boundary

- Research components propose and estimate; they cannot sign promotion.
- AI agents cannot alter context truth, data roles, protected evidence, risk limits, runtime generations or live authorizations.
- Policies remain bounded by UCEE I12 admission, I13 authority/fallback, I14 compilation, I17 risk/portfolio and I18 release qualification.
- Unsupported, stale, incomplete, OOD, mismatched or corrupt paths resolve to Manual, Skip, Abstain, Reject or Quarantine.

# Acceptance checklist

- [ ] All material claims trace to immutable artifacts.
- [ ] Authorities are deny-by-default and independently reviewable.
- [ ] Every promoted policy has kill criteria and rollback generation.
- [ ] The operating model remains viable when best model, period and symbol are removed.

# Required evidence packet

- Specification and assumption cards.
- Code, environment, data and artifact hashes.
- Positive, negative, boundary, mutation and adversarial tests.
- Baseline, ablation, stress, transport and reproducibility reports.
- Open limitations, kill criteria, downstream handoff and rollback path.
