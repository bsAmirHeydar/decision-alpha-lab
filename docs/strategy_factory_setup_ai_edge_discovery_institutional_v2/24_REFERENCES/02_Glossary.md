# Core terms

- **Context:** An upstream UCEE market-state occurrence with immutable known-time evidence.
- **Setup archetype:** A falsifiable hypothesis for exploiting a context.
- **Payoff profile:** The desired shape of loss, hit rate, convexity, tail capture, and capital usage.
- **Entry mechanism:** How the setup pays for confirmation, immediacy, or price improvement.
- **Treatment:** Complete entry, stop, exit, trail, management, expiry, and risk-eligibility composition.
- **Opportunity cluster:** One context occurrence plus all sibling treatments and skip.
- **Outcome cube:** Counterfactual replay results across candidates, paths, economics, brokers, and stresses.
- **Support:** The region where context, views, features, treatments, and domains have adequate evidence.
- **Abstain:** Refusal due to insufficient knowledge/support.
- **Skip:** Supported economic decision not to trade.
- **Promotion admission:** Signed maximum authority/support granted to a policy.
- **Capability tier:** Operational boundary for an AI method.
- **Protected evidence:** Locked final, prospective, live, or incident data whose exposure is controlled.
- **Edge genome:** Machine-readable phenotype of a validated edge.
