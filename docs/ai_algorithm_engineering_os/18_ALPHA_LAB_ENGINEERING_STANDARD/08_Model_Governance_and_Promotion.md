# Model Governance and Promotion

Models are compared on fixed OOS folds against simple baselines. Calibration, error distribution, stability, leakage, and complexity are evaluated before promotion.

Promotion is a separate human decision artifact specifying exact version, feature contract, fallback, monitoring, rollback, and authority. A leaderboard winner does not self-promote.
