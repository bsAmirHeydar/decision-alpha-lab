# Live Execution Readiness Checklist

- [ ] Decision contract is promoted and versioned.
- [ ] Paper/live modes are separated.
- [ ] Risk limits and kill switch are external to model output.
- [ ] Idempotency and duplicate prevention are tested.
- [ ] Startup recovery and broker reconciliation are tested.
- [ ] Stale/missing data fails closed.
- [ ] Monitoring, incident, and rollback paths are active.
- [ ] Capital authority is explicitly approved.
