# Style, Mechanism, and Treatment Separation

## Canonical Separation

- **Payoff Profile:** desired distribution and objective constraints.
- **Entry Mechanism:** how the order becomes active.
- **Trigger Philosophy:** anticipatory, immediate or confirmatory.
- **Treatment Bundle:** executable combination of entry, stop, exit and management.

A Breakout is not a convex strategy by itself. A Wide Stop is not an entry method. A Trail is not a complete setup. These dimensions are crossed only through a compatibility graph.

## Why It Matters

This separation enables:

- controlled search spaces;
- meaningful ablation;
- attribution of uplift;
- reuse across Context families;
- independent cost and fill models;
- explicit path-dependence requirements;
- correct model task routing.

## Identity

The candidate identity includes all axes and every behavior-changing parameter. Two candidates differing in trail activation, order expiry, spread handling or stop buffer are distinct trials.
