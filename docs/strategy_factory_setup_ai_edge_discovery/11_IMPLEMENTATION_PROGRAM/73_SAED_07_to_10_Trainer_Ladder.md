# SAED-07 to SAED-10 — Trainer Ladder

## SAED-07

Eligibility, trigger/fill, basic net-R and calibration tasks using transparent models.

## SAED-08

Grouped ranking and Treatment choice with OOF composition, Skip candidate and manual uplift attribution.

## SAED-09

Survival, competing risks, quantile/distributional outcomes and path/trail suitability.

## SAED-10

Regime specialists, novelty/OOD, multi-view/sequence/graph challengers and bounded ensembles.

## Golden Gate

Every advanced task beats or is rejected against simpler baselines on the same frozen folds and executable costs; unsupported task/view pairs fail before training.
