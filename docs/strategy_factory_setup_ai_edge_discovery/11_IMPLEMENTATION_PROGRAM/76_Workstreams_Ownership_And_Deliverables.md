# Workstreams, Ownership, and Deliverables

## Workstreams

- Domain/Context owner: semantics and archetype mechanism.
- Treatment architect: registries, state machines and compatibility.
- Data/economics engineer: outcomes, fills, costs and datasets.
- ML engineer: task plugins, OOF, calibration and artifacts.
- Research governance: firewall, search ledger and evidence.
- Statistical reviewer: anti-overfit and promotion vetoes.
- Runtime/MQL5 engineer: parity, export and performance.
- Operator/risk owner: portfolio and production authorization.

## Per-Phase Deliverables

Specification, schemas/adapters, implementation, golden/negative fixtures, tests, telemetry, docs, QA, manifest, hashes, residual risks and downstream handoff.
