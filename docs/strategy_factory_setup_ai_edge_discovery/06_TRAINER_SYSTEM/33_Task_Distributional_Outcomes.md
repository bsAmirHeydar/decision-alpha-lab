# Task 3 — Distributional Outcome and Tail Models

## Why Distributional

Mean R alone hides negative skew, multimodality and regime dependence. Predict quantiles or distribution parameters for net R, MFE, MAE, holding time and cost.

## Outputs

- conditional mean/median;
- lower and upper quantiles;
- probability of loss/large winner;
- expected shortfall proxy;
- uncertainty interval;
- calibration by quantile.

## Payoff-Specific Use

P1/P5 emphasize calibrated hit probability and lower bounds. P2/P3 emphasize right-tail probability and magnitude. P4 emphasizes persistence, duration and tail capture.
