# Model Risk and Edge Risk Scorecard

## Dimensions

- semantic/causal risk;
- data quality and support;
- selection/multiplicity risk;
- statistical uncertainty;
- calibration;
- stability/brittleness;
- cost/execution sensitivity;
- tail and drawdown risk;
- model complexity;
- runtime parity/export risk;
- operational/monitoring readiness;
- dependence/capacity risk.

## Rule

Critical blockers remain categorical vetoes. Non-critical dimensions may form a transparent score only after veto evaluation.

## Use

Determine promotion state, paper risk cap, challenger priority, monitoring intensity and retirement thresholds.
