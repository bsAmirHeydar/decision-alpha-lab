# Anti-Overfit Master Protocol

## Defense in Depth

No single statistic proves an edge. Promotion requires simultaneous semantic, causal, economic, statistical, stability, prospective and operational evidence.

## Mandatory Layers

1. Known-time and future-perturbation checks.
2. Clustered purged walk-forward.
3. Nested selection and honest trial universe.
4. Multiplicity correction and deflated performance.
5. PBO/CSCV and winner-overfit diagnostics.
6. White Reality Check / SPA where appropriate.
7. Matched nulls and negative controls.
8. Parameter/feature/treatment stability.
9. Cost, delay, fill and broker stresses.
10. Cross-symbol/feed/regime/time tests.
11. Best-trade/period/cluster removal.
12. Calibration and decision-quality checks.
13. Frozen prospective paper challenge.
14. Runtime parity and shadow evidence.

## Veto Rule

Leakage, incomplete selection accounting, non-reproducibility, critical parity failure or unsafe execution is a hard veto. Strong performance cannot average it away.
