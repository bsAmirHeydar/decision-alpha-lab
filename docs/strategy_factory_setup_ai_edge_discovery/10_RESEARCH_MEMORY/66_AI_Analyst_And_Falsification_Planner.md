# AI Analyst and Falsification Planner

## Analyst Inputs

Only registered machine-generated artifacts: fold reports, candidate outcomes, nulls, stresses, attribution, calibration, incidents and lineage.

## Structured Output

- profit/loss source;
- Context/regime attribution;
- payoff/entry/treatment attribution;
- feature contribution and ablation;
- cost/execution attribution;
- alternative explanations;
- confidence and counter-evidence;
- next falsification experiment.

## Authority

The analyst cannot promote, modify tested artifacts or convert speculation into fact. Every numeric claim links to evidence.
