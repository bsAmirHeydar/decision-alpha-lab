# CG Chapter 08 — Decision Map

```text
Does a same-day reference exist?
↓
No → no divergence context
Yes
↓
Did one symbol hunt the reference?
↓
No → wait
Yes
↓
Did the other symbol also hunt its corresponding reference?
↓
Yes → divergence invalid
No
↓
Has the active candle closed?
↓
No → potential divergence only
Yes
↓
Does asymmetry still exist?
↓
No → invalid
Yes
↓
Confirmed divergence
↓
Trade permission
↓
Display and preserve signal
```

## Principle

The close does not create the hunt. The close grants permission to judge the hunt.
