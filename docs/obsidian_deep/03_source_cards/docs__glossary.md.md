## Summary

A structural high or low where the market previously made a visible decision. A finite territory around a node. The project avoids treating structural nodes as infinitesimal points. The observable force associated with a structural event. It may express as rejection, continuation, hunt, break, velocity, or path quality. A state or event where price interaction with a structural zone is interpreted as self-correcting behavior. A state or event where price interaction with a structural zone is interpreted as self-reinforcing behavior. The current dominant market state: reversal pressure, continuation pressure, or ambiguity. The candle/time at which a label or event becomes knowable without future data. All events or labels that become knowable on the same candle/time.
