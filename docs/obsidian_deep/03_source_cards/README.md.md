## Summary

Decision Alpha Lab is a structural market research laboratory. Its purpose is not to fit indicators to past price, but to test whether price contains repeatable decision structure around confirmed market nodes. The project has evolved into a strict research program built around three contracts: **Structural nodes instead of fixed time windows** — the x-axis of the market is not a calendar window; it is a sequence of decision nodes, zones, revisits, breaks, hunts, and confirmed state changes. **Live-known time instead of completed-sample ordering** — a regime may only influence a later decision after the candle/time at which it became knowable. Events that become known on the same candle are simultaneous, not… **Execution R instead of path-normalized R** — an R multiple is only tradable when a real initial risk model exists: zone edge, ATR stop, fixed R, trailing stop, or another explicit
