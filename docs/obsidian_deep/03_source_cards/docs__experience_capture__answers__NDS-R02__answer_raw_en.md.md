Hook and Cycle are the same thing technically and algorithmically. They are only two viewpoints or two names for one thing. Every node can be the start of a Cycle/Hook, even small nodes. A Cycle starts from a node. It moves toward an Extreme and then comes back toward near-death, or ND. The Extreme between two nodes means the highest price the market has seen between those two nodes. In the negative/inverted case, the opposite logic applies. ND means price comes near the origin of the Cycle/Hook.
