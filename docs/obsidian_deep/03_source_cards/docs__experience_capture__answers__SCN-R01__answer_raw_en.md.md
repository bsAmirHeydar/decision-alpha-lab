## Summary

Context power, position, exploitable constraints, training targets, reversal probability, X/Y closure, symmetry confluence, destination openness, optionality, parent-child dominance, and trade-zone promotion. The amount of power and even the quality of it must be trained. In our viewpoint, some things are already clear, and some things are two-state or multi-state. Those ambiguous parts need training. There are two main places where training is needed. First: which movement constraint is more exploitable with lower cost? That means lower risk for larger and longer profits. Second: when there is no single rule and the situation has multiple possible states, which states should we consider so that we pay less cost and make more profit?
