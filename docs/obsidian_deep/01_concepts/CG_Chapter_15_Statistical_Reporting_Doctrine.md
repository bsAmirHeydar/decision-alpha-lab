# CG Chapter 15 — Statistical Reporting Doctrine

Chapter 15 defines the reporting language of EXP0017.

The core principle: only confirmed and tradeable signals enter the primary statistical report, and every such signal must carry enough information to be classified later.

The report must preserve win rate, R:R, pip movement, daily-range-normalized movement, dollar outcome, cycle-group type, overlap, and signal frequency.

This chapter is the bridge between raw strategy doctrine and later statistical truth.
