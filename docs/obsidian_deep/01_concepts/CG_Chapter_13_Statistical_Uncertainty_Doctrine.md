# CG Chapter 13 — Statistical Uncertainty Doctrine

Chapter 13 defines uncertainty as the correct base state of the strategy.

The architect does not want to invent quality distinctions before statistical testing. This means the strategy should not rank, filter, or favor signal families until the data shows which differences matter.

## Core principle

> Unknown is not weakness. Unknown is a protected research state.

## Operational meaning

Every raw divergence remains valid for study unless it is invalidated by the core divergence logic itself.

## What this note prevents

- premature filters;
- subjective signal quality;
- visual hierarchy;
- CG mythology;
- symbol-role bias;
- direction bias;
- time-window bias;
- reference-distance bias.
