# Temporal Close, Not Price Filter

## Core Doctrine

Chapter 08 says confirmation is based on **time close**, not on a special close position.

## Meaning

The candle must finish, but it does not need to close beyond the hunted level.

## Not Required

- close above hunted high,
- close below hunted low,
- rejection close,
- body dominance,
- displacement close,
- candle-color confirmation.

## Required

- the active candle closes,
- one symbol has hunted,
- the other symbol has not hunted,
- the asymmetry remains alive.

## Strategic Purpose

The strategy wants a completed time unit, not a separate price-action filter.
