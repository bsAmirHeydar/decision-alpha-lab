# Close Is Not Late Doctrine

## Core Idea

Waiting for candle close is not considered late in this strategy.

## Interpretation

The strategy values permission over speed.

## Why

A hunt inside an open candle is not enough. The relationship between the two symbols must survive until the candle closes.

## Strategic Contrast

| Early-Reaction Thinking | Chapter 08 Thinking |
|---|---|
| Enter immediately after touch. | Wait for the behavioral unit to finish. |
| Speed is edge. | Confirmation is discipline. |
| Close is delay. | Close is permission. |
