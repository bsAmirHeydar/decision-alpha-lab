# Multiple Positions From One CG Allowed

A cycle group may create more than one valid signal. The base doctrine allows all of them.

## Why

A CG is a family of divergence behavior, not a one-trade container.

## Statistical questions

- Is first signal better than later signals?
- Are repeated signals useful?
- Do repeated signals create overexposure?
- Does same-CG clustering increase or reduce expectancy?

No answer is assumed before testing.
