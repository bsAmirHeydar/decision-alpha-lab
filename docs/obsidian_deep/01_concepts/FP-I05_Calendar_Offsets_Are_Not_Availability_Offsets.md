# FP-I05 Calendar Offsets Are Not Availability Offsets

## Definition

Calendar offset 2 always means the date exactly two days before the anchor, even when that date has no valid N window.

## Operational consequence

The rule is represented in a closed contract, included in semantic identity, exercised by tests, and preserved in the FP-I06 handoff.
