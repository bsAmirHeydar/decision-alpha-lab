# Confirmation Families as Statistical Discovery

## Core Idea

The base strategy does not differentiate confirmation candle types. But later statistics may discover useful confirmation families.

## Possible Families

- early-cycle confirmation,
- mid-cycle confirmation,
- late-cycle confirmation,
- large-range confirmation candle,
- small-range confirmation candle,
- confirmation near reference,
- confirmation far from reference,
- fast confirmation after hunt,
- delayed confirmation after hunt,
- cash-session confirmation,
- outside-cash confirmation.

## Doctrine

These are not filters yet. They are possible study categories.
