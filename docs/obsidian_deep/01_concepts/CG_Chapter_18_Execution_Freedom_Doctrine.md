# CG Chapter 18 — Execution Freedom Doctrine

Chapter 18 defines execution freedom as a base research state. Every confirmed and non-invalidated divergence is allowed to express itself as a tradeable event.

## Core principle

No manual restriction is added before statistics.

## Protected assumptions

- no maximum position count;
- no maximum same-CG count;
- no hedge ban;
- no post-loss ban;
- no priority between CGs;
- no manual conflict resolution;
- no delay after confirmation.

## Why this exists

The strategy must first understand its raw signal field. Restrictions added before measurement can hide the real behavior of the strategy.
