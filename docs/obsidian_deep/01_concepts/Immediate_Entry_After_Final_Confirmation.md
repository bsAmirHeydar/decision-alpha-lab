# Immediate Entry After Final Confirmation

Entry occurs immediately after the candle closes and the divergence remains valid.

## Not required before entry

- retest;
- pullback;
- extra candle;
- manual confirmation;
- news filter;
- trend filter;
- model approval.

## Meaning

The confirmed divergence itself is the permission boundary.

## Research value

Immediate entry creates a clean baseline: what happens after the signal becomes valid?
