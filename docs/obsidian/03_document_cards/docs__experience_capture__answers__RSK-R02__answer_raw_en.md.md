## خلاصه

Risk coefficients, account-risk percentage, true cost including stop and commission, high reward target, and multi-level optionality. Risks have a coefficient based on the quality of the setups. By risk I mean the percentage risk of the account. Then it must be adjusted in such a way that, considering stop distance and commission, the exact cost becomes that same number. These are the costs. We are looking for rewards above 10R. Also, this optionality discussion should not be considered only at the final entry. At the context level, zone level, and entry level, optionality must be examined at each level.
