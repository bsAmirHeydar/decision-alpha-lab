## خلاصه

Path: Summary: A Potential Zone in NDS is a price range where multi-sided and fractal analysis shows that the current structural position is exploitable with lower cost and larger explosion potential. Context tells the situation; zone tells where that situation can be used. A zone may be created from many combinations of NDS constraints, including Hook completion, CycleHook near-death, F-counting relations such as F2 >= F1, X/Y closure, symmetry confluence, origin boundaries, and destination structure. Once a zone exists, exact entry is refined on a lower timeframe through an entry-level Extreme, where a lower-scale Hook closes as much as possible and allows the smallest practical stop. Zone
