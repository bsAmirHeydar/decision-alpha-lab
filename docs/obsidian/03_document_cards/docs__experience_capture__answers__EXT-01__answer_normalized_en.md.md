## خلاصه

Extreme is not the node itself. Extreme is the near-death zone around an untouched node. It is the area where price approaches the point at which a cycle would die, but before the cycle is fully killed. The value of Extreme comes from: Extreme must not become the whole entry layer. It is one entry family among multiple possible NDS-native entry frameworks. The system should eventually support an entry-family architecture such as: Extreme may be one of the most logical and convex entries, but it must remain one entry type. A cycle starts from a node.
