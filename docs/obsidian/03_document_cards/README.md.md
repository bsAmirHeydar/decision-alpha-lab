## خلاصه

Decision Alpha Lab is a structural market research laboratory. Its purpose is not to fit indicators to past price, but to test whether price contains repeatable decision structure around confirmed market nodes. The project has evolved into a strict research program built around three contracts: 1. **Structural nodes instead of fixed time windows** — the x-axis of the market is not a calendar window; it is a sequence of decision nodes, zones, revisits, breaks, hunts, and confirmed state changes. 2. **Live-known time instead of completed-sample ordering** — a regime may only influence a later decision after the candle/time at which it became knowable.
