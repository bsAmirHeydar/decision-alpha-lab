## خلاصه

Canonical NDS object model, axes, fractality, timeframe dominance, and symmetry. X-axis: Hook → Node-counting. Y-axis: Rally → F-counting. I already explained the rules for all of them. Fractality means doing the same thing across different timeframes. Higher timeframes dominate lower timeframes. Also include symmetries. In F-counting, F2 is larger than F1 or at least equal to F1. In Hook node-counting, Hooks 1, 2, and 3 usually have symmetry. If Hook 4 happens, Hook 4 may go beyond the symmetry. This symmetry can help with entry points, zone definition, and improving the accuracy of Extreme entries.
