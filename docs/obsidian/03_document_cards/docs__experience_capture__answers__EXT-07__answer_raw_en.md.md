## خلاصه

Multiple nearby L2 nodes and selected Extreme anchor logic. Each one of these nodes is an entry opportunity. Because we operate based on convexity, win rate is not our criterion. Our training and our strategy are based on positive expectancy and on how well we can find places that have good potential and trade them. So prediction is not important to us. It is also not important for us to say that we have found a formula for the market. We look for explosion potential and reward potential, and we trade those opportunities.
