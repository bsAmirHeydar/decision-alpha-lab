## خلاصه

Risk budgeting across context, zone, and entry levels; measuring costliness, cleanliness, aggregate tolerated risk, reward tradeoff, and trainable scoring. Measure the risks at each level and see how much risk we should spend. At the context level. At the zone level. At the entry level. See which contexts are expensive to make decisions from, and which contexts are much cleaner and lower cost. Do the same for zones and entries. This must be trained completely so we can see what level of risk we are tolerating in aggregate in exchange for what profit and what reward. This way we can score them.
