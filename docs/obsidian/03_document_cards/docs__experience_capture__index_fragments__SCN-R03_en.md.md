## خلاصه

Path: Summary: In NDS, multiple scenarios do not need to collapse into one dominant truth. Each scenario is evaluated by its own NDS evidence and by whether it offers a convex trade: lower cost, larger possible reward, wider reward path, and more open optionality. Potential matters more than correctness. Every scenario should be continuously tested, but ranking should first prioritize convexity and cost-to-potential quality. Win rate matters only after convexity has been established. The system should output a live Convex Opportunity Set containing primary, secondary, watchlist, conditional, and vetoed scenario threads.
