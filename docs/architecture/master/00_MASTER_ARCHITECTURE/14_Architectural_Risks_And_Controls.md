# Architectural Risks and Controls

## 1. False Discovery From Search Scale

### Risk
AI can test enough setups to find spectacular noise.

### Controls
Complete trial ledger, hierarchical multiple-testing corrections, PBO/deflated metrics, nulls, stability tests and prospective evidence.

## 2. Test-Set Feedback Contamination

### Risk
AI Analyst or human redesign based on final-test results turns test into training.

### Controls
Research Firewall, exposure ledger, immutable data roles and new lineage after exposure.

## 3. Context Redefinition After Results

### Risk
The context is edited until it becomes profitable.

### Controls
Doctrine freeze, semantic versioning, migration and separate evidence lineage.

## 4. Metric Gaming

### Risk
High win-rate creates tiny targets and huge tail risk; high reward creates unusably sparse trades.

### Controls
Multi-objective constrained optimization, sample/coverage guardrails, tail and cost limits.

## 5. Money-Management Overfit

### Risk
Sizing rules are optimized on the same history and appear to improve edge.

### Controls
Separate capital research and validation, simple baselines, Monte Carlo and portfolio tests.

## 6. Strategy-Specific Duplication

### Risk
Every context builds its own outcome, statistics, execution and monitoring.

### Controls
Plugin contracts, Context SDK, treatment compiler, shared evidence and golden-slice discipline.

## 7. Over-Generalization

### Risk
A universal engine erases domain meaning.

### Controls
Modularize mechanisms, not semantics. Context-specific doctrine and transition rules remain first-class.

## 8. Silent Live Adaptation

### Risk
Auto-retraining or threshold drift mutates production.

### Controls
Immutable runtime generations, shadow/challenger, explicit promotion and rollback.

## 9. AI Analyst Hallucination

### Risk
Convincing explanation becomes accepted causal truth.

### Controls
Artifact-grounded claims, confidence, counter-evidence, falsification proposals and non-authoritative status.

## 10. Portfolio Blindness

### Risk
Individually valid strategies create concentrated joint exposure.

### Controls
Edge genome, shared-event clusters, exposure mapping, reservations and portfolio risk authority.

## 11. Security Breaking Safety

### Risk
License or remote-service failure prevents safe management of open trades.

### Controls
Degraded safe mode, local hard risk, independent kill and safe exit capability.

## 12. Documentation Becoming Detached

### Risk
Documents become aspirational and code diverges.

### Controls
Machine-readable specs, generated manifests, executable fixtures, contract tests and linked artifacts.

## 13. Complexity Overwhelming Speed

### Risk
The platform becomes harder to understand than rebuilding strategies.

### Controls
Golden paths, clear ownership, module KPIs, deletion/deprecation cycles, automated artifact generation and measurement of marginal context cost.
