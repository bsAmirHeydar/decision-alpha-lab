# Research Firewall and Evidence

## 1. Why the Firewall Is Central

A powerful AI search system can manufacture convincing false edges if it repeatedly sees validation and test results. The Research Firewall controls who and what may observe each data role.

## 2. Protected Data Roles

```text
Exploration
→ Training
→ Calibration
→ Selection Validation
→ Locked Final Test
→ Prospective Paper
→ Micro-Live
→ Live
```

The final test is a consumable asset. Once viewed, summarized or used to alter the policy, it cannot remain pristine for the same lineage.

## 3. Exposure Ledger

Every protected access records:

- actor or process;
- experiment lineage;
- data and fields exposed;
- time and purpose;
- prompt or downstream decision;
- whether the dataset keeps its prior role.

AI-generated recommendations based on final-test outcomes count as exposure.

## 4. Experiment Compiler

Before a run, the compiler freezes:

- falsifiable hypothesis;
- context versions;
- treatment universe;
- outcome and label policy;
- cost profiles;
- data cutoff;
- fold and cluster plan;
- model ladder;
- objective and guardrails;
- trial budget and stopping;
- metrics and evidence criteria.

The compiler produces an immutable run plan and reproduction command.

## 5. Trial Ledger

Every attempt is retained:

- completed;
- failed;
- timed out;
- pruned;
- duplicate;
- invalid;
- cancelled.

Without the complete trial universe, multiple-testing and winner-selection adjustments are dishonest.

## 6. Evidence Lifecycle

Evidence moves through explicit states rather than pass/fail. Promotion depends on semantic correctness, net economics, uncertainty, false-discovery controls, stability, prospective parity, capacity and operational readiness.

## 7. Deterministic Gate Versus AI Analyst

The evidence gate applies machine-readable rules. The AI Analyst explains why profit or loss may have occurred and proposes new experiments. The analyst cannot promote a policy by narrative.

## 8. Failure as Output

Rejection is successful research when it is reproducible and its reason is retained. Failed contexts, treatments and models reduce future search cost through Failure Memory.
