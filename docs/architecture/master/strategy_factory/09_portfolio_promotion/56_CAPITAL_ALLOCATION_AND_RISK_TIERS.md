# Capital Allocation and Risk Tiers

Capital allocation is downstream of validated edge, execution fidelity, and dependence—not a reward for model confidence.

## Tiers

Research/paper, micro-live, normal, scaled, and quarantined. Each tier has risk ceilings, evidence requirements, and rollback triggers. Scale changes gradually in risk units and unique-cluster experience.

## Allocation inputs

OOS lower confidence bound, drawdown, tail dependence, live cost error, calibration, capacity, strategy overlap, and operational reliability. Expected return alone is insufficient.

## Anti-procyclical rule

Do not scale immediately after a short winning run. Scaling requires predeclared review windows and evidence across regimes. Losses beyond tolerance reduce or halt risk automatically.
