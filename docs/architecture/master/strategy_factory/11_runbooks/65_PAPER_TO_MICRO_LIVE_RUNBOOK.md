# Runbook — Paper to Micro-Live

This runbook closes the gap between historical evidence and actual execution.

## Paper checklist

Live event parity, feature completeness, inference latency, deterministic candidate identity, risk gate, restart recovery, broker-session simulation, trace completeness, and no unauthorized sends.

## Micro-live checklist

Separate account or bounded allocation, tiny fixed risk, approved symbols/sessions, live bridge compile/test, emergency stop, operator availability, and signed promotion. Initial goal is execution fidelity, not income.

## Review

Compare every live intent with research and paper: event, features, decision, price, stop, target, costs, and result. Material discrepancy triggers demotion.
