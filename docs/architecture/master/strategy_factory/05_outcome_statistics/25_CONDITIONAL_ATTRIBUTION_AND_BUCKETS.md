# Conditional Attribution and Bucket Analysis

Bucket analysis is a discovery instrument, not automatic filtering authority.

## Bucket construction

Continuous features use train-defined bins or economically meaningful thresholds. Categories require minimum clusters. Buckets are compared with confidence intervals and trial accounting.

## Simpson and interaction risk

A strong aggregate may reverse inside regimes, and a strong bucket may merely proxy time or volatility. Cross-tabs, partial models, and ablations are required before interpreting causality.

## Promotion of a bucket

A bucket becomes a rule only after it is frozen, tested on untouched periods, survives FDR and cost stress, and shows a plausible mechanism. Otherwise it stays in the research ledger.
