# ACL-OS Controlled Vocabulary

Canonical terms prevent semantic drift across code, documents and reports.

## Core terms

**Context**: deterministic market condition independent of exploitation. **Occurrence**: one identified manifestation of a Context. **Setup**: proposed exploitation behavior. **Treatment**: canonical executable behavior identity. **Policy**: selection, abstention or risk decision over Treatments. **Batch**: immutable research execution definition. **Trial**: one evaluated candidate within a Batch. **Promotion**: governed lifecycle decision. **Runtime generation**: immutable signed execution bundle. **Experience**: structured lesson derived from evidence. **Doctrine**: human-authorized semantic specification.

## Evidence terms

**Observed** is directly recorded. **Derived** follows deterministic transformation. **Estimated** uses statistical/model assumptions. **Prospective** is produced after freeze on unseen future data. **Operational** comes from paper, shadow or live infrastructure. **Authorized** is an explicit decision, not a metric.

## Security terms

**Trust zone**, **capability**, **attestation**, **provenance**, **SBOM**, **quarantine**, **revocation**, **recall**, **kill switch**, **break glass** and **residual risk** use the meanings in the security domain notes.
