# 14 Handoff to Research

Win Rate, strongest family, optimal cycle boundaries, optimal lookback, forecast horizon, reversal probability, and all Entry or Execution designs belong to a separate research program.

Research may consume immutable Context events and propose new versioned cycle definitions, but it may not overwrite the accepted Context package or redefine historical events in place.
