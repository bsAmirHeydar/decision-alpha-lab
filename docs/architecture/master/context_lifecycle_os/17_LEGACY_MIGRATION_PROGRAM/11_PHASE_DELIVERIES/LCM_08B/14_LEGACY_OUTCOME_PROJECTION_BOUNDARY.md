# Legacy Outcome Projection Boundary

MFE, MAE, return, and bars-to-extreme are research outcome projections, not context state. They remain in the compatibility adapter solely to reproduce the legacy event schema. They are excluded from the canonical occurrence contract and cannot acquire treatment or execution authority.
