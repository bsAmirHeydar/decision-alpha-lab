The claim ceiling is `LCM_16B_RECOVERY_AND_PROGRAM_CLOSURE_CONTROL`. Scope includes the migration control plane, the exact LCM-16A handoff, local recovery and rehydration, the 30-subphase final ledger, locator continuity, residual risk, authority/deletion final state, external-evidence intake and human closure approval.
