# Scope, Non-Goals and Claim Ceiling

## In scope

Domain source, supporting research code, configurations, fixtures, visual projections, execution adapters, documentation, aliases, redirects, quarantine and deletion evidence.

## Out of scope without a new decision

Strategy optimization, parameter search, capital promotion, live broker activation, model training, Product Lab migration and rewriting ACL-OS internals.

## Claim ceiling

`LEGACY_MIGRATION_REFERENCE_ONLY`

A successful migration proves traceability and bounded parity in the tested environments. It does not prove alpha, economic equivalence under all brokers, production security or permission to trade.
