# Automated Executive Summary

The executive summary is answer-first but cannot compress away uncertainty. It contains aggregate decisions, the claim ceiling, principal limitations and authority denials. Candidate identifiers and private logic are excluded from executive and external views unless the audience policy explicitly permits them.

The summary may state that evidence is insufficient, validation failed, or evidence is eligible for reporting. It may never translate those states into alpha, deployment, position sizing, order or capital language.
