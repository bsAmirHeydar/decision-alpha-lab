# Novelty and Material Change Classifier

ACL-08 calculates a reproducible report diff, not scientific novelty. The classifier detects material changes in evidence identity and outcome categories. It does not declare a new market mechanism, a novel strategy or an improvement.

Research novelty, duplicate prevention and expected information value belong to ACL-09, which consumes the diff and experience fingerprints under a separate authority permit.
