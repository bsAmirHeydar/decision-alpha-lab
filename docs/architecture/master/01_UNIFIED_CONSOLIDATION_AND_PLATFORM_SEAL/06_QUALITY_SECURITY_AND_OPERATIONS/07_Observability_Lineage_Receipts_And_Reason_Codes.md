# Observability, Lineage, Receipts and Reason Codes

## Observability model

Logs describe events, metrics describe health, traces describe flow and receipts prove governed actions. Each uses stable run, artifact and Context identities.

## Reason codes

Failures, abstentions, transitions, policy rejections, data-quality states and cutover decisions use machine-readable reason codes with human explanations.

## Lineage

Every generated, trained, evaluated and runtime artifact traces to authored contract, compiler, policy, source data, task, environment and approval.

## Monitoring

Operational monitoring detects data, model, assumption, runtime, execution and infrastructure health. It does not silently grant retraining or capital authority.
