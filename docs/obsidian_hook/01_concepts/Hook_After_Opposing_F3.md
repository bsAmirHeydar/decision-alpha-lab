# Hook After Opposing F3

A Hook After Opposing F3 is a hook that appears after an F3 structure in the opposite direction.

Example:

- Bearish F3 prints.
- A bullish hook forms after it.
- That bullish hook is valid because it emerges from the reversal or exhaustion field created by the opposing F3.

## Rule

> F3 creates broad potential. The opposing hook creates the measurable risk contract.

## Zone Implication

The F3 itself may be too broad to trade directly. The opposing hook after F3 may create the tradable zone.
