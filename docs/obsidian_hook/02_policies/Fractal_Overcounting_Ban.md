# Fractal Overcounting Ban

The system must not accept hooks merely because they can be counted on some fractal scale.

Fractal interpretation is useful only when it leads to measurable risk. Otherwise, it creates noise.

## Policy

- Do not count arbitrary internal swings as valid hooks.
- Do not re-anchor hooks after seeing outcome.
- Do not accept hook-like structures without structural lineage.
- Do not allow invalid hook zones into the primary entry engine.

## Principle

> Fractal awareness is useful. Fractal over-counting is destructive.
