# Hook Terminal Must Stay Inside Origin Boundary

A Hook terminal cannot cross its own origin boundary.

For a positive Hook, the visual terminal is the lowest raw low after the crown, but it must remain above origin.

For a negative Hook, the visual terminal is the highest raw high after the crown, but it must remain below origin.

If price touches or crosses origin, terminal scanning stops. Anything beyond that belongs to Hook death or invalidation, not to the living cycle endpoint.
