# Canonical Hook Validity Determination

Valid Hook families are determined after structural Hook counting.

## Valid families

1. Hook After Opposing F3
2. Hook After Hook

## Hook After Opposing F3

A valid post-F3 Hook must oppose the F3 direction and start from the F3 terminal side.

The F3 may be completed or locked. The terminal endpoint may be the extension end, confirm node, or leg2 node depending on what the F3 has already produced.

## Hook After Hook

Hook-2 is valid when it starts from the structural terminal node of Hook-1 and both Hooks are the same genus.

Genus means same direction.

## Important separation

Structural terminal node id is for continuity.
Raw terminal price/time is for drawing.
