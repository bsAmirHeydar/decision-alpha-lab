# Hook Zone Eligibility Policy

A hook zone is eligible only when:

1. The hook source is valid.
2. The entry boundary is defined.
3. The invalidation boundary is defined.
4. Zone width is acceptable or can be refined through a child zone.
5. The context offers open reward potential.
6. The trade can be expressed as a bounded-risk, open-reward limit entry.

If any of these are missing, the zone becomes a watch zone, not a direct trade zone.
