# Post F3 Hook Families

Valid post-F3 Hook recognition has four canonical families.

```text
F3H_DIRECT_STRUCTURAL
F3H_DIRECT_GEOMETRIC_80
F3H_DELAYED_STRUCTURAL
F3H_DELAYED_GEOMETRIC_80
```

## Direct families

The Hook starts at or near the F3 terminal side.

## Delayed/rebound families

The Hook forms after price reaches the F3 terminal side and rebounds away.

## Structural families

Require full Hook sequence and confirmed terminal node.

## Geometric families

Require explicit 80% cycle mode and threshold qualification.
