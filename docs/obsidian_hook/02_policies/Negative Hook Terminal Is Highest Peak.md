# Negative Hook Terminal Is Highest Peak

A negative Hook ends at the highest same-side peak reached by its Hook origin group.

It does not end merely at the latest compact display label or at an arbitrary sequence row.

Mirror rule:

- positive Hook terminal = lowest valley;
- negative Hook terminal = highest peak.

The terminal must remain below the Hook origin boundary. Touching or crossing origin belongs to Hook death/invalidation rather than a living terminal.

This raw-price terminal is the Phase 52 Sell Limit anchor for a valid negative `HH`, `F3H`, or `F3H+HH` source.
