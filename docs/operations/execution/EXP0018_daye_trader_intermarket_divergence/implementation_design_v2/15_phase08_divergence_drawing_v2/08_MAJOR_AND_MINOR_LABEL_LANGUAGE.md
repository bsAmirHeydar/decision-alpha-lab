# Major and Minor Label Language

The six major relationships may display `WW`, `DD`, `PA`, `AL`, `LN`, or `NP`. The sixteen minor relationships remain line-only.

The trend line remains the signal geometry. A companion midpoint `OBJ_TEXT` is used only to guarantee a visible centered label for major relationships. It carries no independent strategy meaning. Disabling midpoint text preserves the alias in the trend-line description field.
