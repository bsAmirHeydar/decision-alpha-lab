# Scope and Authority

P04 owns only the topology of Daye relationships and the deterministic resolution of current/reference period instances.

## P04 may

- define the canonical set of 22 records;
- identify major/minor family;
- resolve previous-same-code and previous-chronological links;
- verify current/reference period codes and completeness;
- publish typed READY or unavailable resolution records;
- preserve doctrine blockers as machine-readable states.

## P04 may not

- compare highs or lows;
- assign hunter/protected roles;
- assign BUY/SELL direction;
- confirm or invalidate SMT;
- draw chart objects;
- size risk or place orders;
- silently enable WW or NP while their ADRs remain unaccepted.
