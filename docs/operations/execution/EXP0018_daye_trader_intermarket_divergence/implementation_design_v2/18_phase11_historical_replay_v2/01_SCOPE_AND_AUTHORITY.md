# Scope and authority

P11 owns historical reconstruction and equivalence evidence. It may read historical closed bars, build as-of snapshots, replay state transitions, and emit immutable ledgers. It may not reinterpret doctrine, alter P04 mappings, change P05 touch rules, bypass P06 close confirmation, mutate P07 retirement, draw chart objects, or trade.
