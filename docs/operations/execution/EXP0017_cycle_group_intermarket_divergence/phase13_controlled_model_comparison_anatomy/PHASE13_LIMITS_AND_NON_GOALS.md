# Phase 13 Limits and Non-Goals

Phase 13 does not:

- place or simulate broker orders;
- change risk sizing;
- change stop or target rules;
- disable cycle groups;
- filter live signals;
- optimize on the test window;
- use future-derived features;
- claim causal explanations from coefficients;
- deploy a production model;
- grant AI or model execution authority.

The standard-library models are deliberate research baselines, not claims of optimal machine learning.
