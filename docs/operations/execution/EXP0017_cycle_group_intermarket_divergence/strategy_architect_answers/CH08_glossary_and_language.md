# CH08 — Glossary and Strategy Language

## Candle Close

The moment the active candle finishes. In Chapter 08, candle close is the boundary that allows the strategy to judge whether a divergence is confirmed.

## Confirmation Candle

The candle whose close confirms that the hunter-clean asymmetry still exists.

## Temporal Close

A close used as a time boundary rather than a price-location requirement.

This means the candle only needs to finish. It does not need to close above or below the hunted reference.

## Potential Divergence

A forming condition where one symbol has hunted and the other has not, but the active candle has not closed yet.

## Confirmed Divergence

A divergence that survives until candle close. One symbol has hunted, the other has not, and the active candle has completed.

## Trade Permission

The right to treat a confirmed divergence as actionable in the base strategy language.

Trade permission does not automatically answer future portfolio-allocation questions.

## Double Hunt

A condition where both symbols hunt their corresponding reference. In the base divergence doctrine, this cancels the divergence.

## Simultaneous Hunt

A double hunt occurring inside the same decision window. It invalidates divergence because there is no remaining intermarket asymmetry.

## Invalidation

The cancellation of a divergence because the clean symbol also hunts its corresponding reference.

## Same Confirmation Standard

The principle that every CG, symbol, direction, and reference family uses the same base confirmation rule before statistical evidence justifies any difference.

## Signal Visibility

The doctrine that all confirmed divergences should be shown and preserved for review and statistical study.

## Equal Base Validity

The rule that confirmed divergences are not manually ranked or weakened before statistical study.

## Close Is Not Late

The strategic belief that waiting for candle close is not a delay but a necessary permission boundary.

## Price-Location Filter

A rule that requires the candle to close in a specific price location. Chapter 08 does not use such a filter in the base layer.

## Confirmation Family

A possible future statistical category based on how a candle confirmed the divergence. Examples include early-cycle confirmation, late-cycle confirmation, large-range confirmation, small-range confirmation, or confirmation near a reference.
