# CH13 — Statistical Uncertainty and No Manual Quality Doctrine

## Core chapter identity

Chapter 13 is the doctrine of **not knowing before testing**.

The strategy architect repeatedly answers that many potential distinctions are not known yet and must be tested statistically. This is not a weak answer. It is a hard strategic position:

- do not invent superiority before data;
- do not rank signals visually before data;
- do not treat a possible idea as a rule;
- do not filter raw divergences before measuring them;
- do not assign quality to a CG, time window, direction, symbol role, reference distance, or signal structure without statistical evidence.
