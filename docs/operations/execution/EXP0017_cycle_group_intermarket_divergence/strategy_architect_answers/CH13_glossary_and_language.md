# CH13 — Glossary and Language

## Statistical uncertainty

The state where a difference may exist but has not yet been proven.

## Unknown-before-tested

The doctrine that a claim is not a trading rule until tested statistically.

## No manual quality

The refusal to label a signal as high quality or low quality before statistical evidence.

## Descriptive field

A recorded attribute of a signal that does not yet affect validity, risk, priority, or execution.

Examples:

- CG name;
- time window;
- direction;
- hunter symbol;
- clean symbol;
- reference distance;
- invalidation speed.

## Decision field

A field that has proven statistical value and may affect later decisions.

## Preclassification

Judging a signal before the statistics are collected.

## Postclassification

Classifying a signal family after statistical evidence exists.

## Non-actionable hypothesis

An idea that is preserved for research but does not affect current base decisions.

## Idea backlog

A structured place for future ideas that may later become filters, weights, model features, or separate signal families.

## Neutral baseline

The raw field where all valid divergences are included without manual ranking or filtering.

## Statistical authority

The principle that data, not narrative, decides whether a difference matters.

## Rule promotion

The future process of converting a proven hypothesis into a rule.

## Rule rejection

The future process of discarding or downgrading an idea because it lacks statistical value.

## Raw signal preservation

Keeping all valid raw signals in the research field so the true edge can be measured.
