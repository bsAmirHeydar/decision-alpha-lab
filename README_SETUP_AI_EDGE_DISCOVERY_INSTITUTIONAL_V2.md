# Mission

SAED V2 is the post-Context intelligence, research, evidence and learning architecture for Alpha Lab. It converts immutable UCEE context occurrences into a finite lattice of complete treatment candidates, reconstructs executable counterfactual outcomes, trains a governed model ladder, attempts to falsify every candidate, and hands only support-audited policies into UCEE I12–I18.

# Deep technology stack

- Event-sourced and bitemporal market truth.
- Finite treatment lattice and multi-objective Pareto governance.
- Path-aware fill, impact, stop, target and trail replay.
- Self-supervised multi-view pretraining.
- Transformers, state-space/Mamba and temporal graph challengers.
- Time-series foundation-model intake with contamination and supply-chain controls.
- Distributional, survival, competing-risk and treatment-ranking learning.
- Doubly robust, orthogonal and heterogeneous causal treatment estimation.
- Hierarchical Bayesian meta-learning with negative-transfer controls.
- World models for falsification and synthetic stress—not alpha proof.
- Conservative offline policy learning over finite approved actions.
- Deep ensembles, OOD, conformal/selective risk control and abstention.
- Distributed HPO/compute allocation with complete trial/exposure accounting.
- Multi-agent research control plane with no promotion or trading authority.
- Independent anti-overfit red team, prospective paper and signed promotion.
- Immutable runtime compilation, MQL5 parity, portfolio/capacity and production qualification.
- Research memory, failure graph and active experiment planning.

# Capability tiers

1. **Core Production** — deterministic, calibrated, reproducible and runtime-bounded.
2. **Governed Challenger** — stronger evidence, model-risk and shadow requirements.
3. **Research-Only** — representation, stress or policy research; no direct promotion/live use.
4. **Prohibited-to-Live** — self-modifying models, direct LLM/agent trading, silent retraining and unsigned promotion.
