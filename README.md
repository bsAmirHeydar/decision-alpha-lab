# Decision Alpha Lab

Decision Alpha Lab is a quantitative research platform designed to investigate whether market regime transitions can be inferred from structural decision nodes and their associated energy.

Rather than predicting prices, this project aims to understand how markets transition between self-reinforcing (momentum) and self-correcting (mean-reverting) dynamics, and whether such transitions can be exploited through convex execution frameworks.

## Core Research Questions

- Can decision nodes be systematically extracted from price?
- Can decision energy be quantified?
- Can regime transitions be detected from structural information?
- Do these transitions contain predictive information?
- Can they improve convex trading systems?

## Philosophy

Price is the only directly observable truth.

Markets are collective decision systems that continuously oscillate between momentum and mean-reverting forces.

The objective is not prediction, but contextual adaptation and convexity preservation.
